Cartouche now renews itself and connects LocalDevVPN by itself, your Switch games continue on your iPhone, Siri starts your games, and your Mac plays them with your iPhone's saves.

- Renewal: each time you open Cartouche in the last 3 days of its signature, it now renews itself as Renew now does, without you. System shows it under way, and why it failed, with its report; it tries again 6 hours later. Until now only Renew now, a Shortcuts automation or an update renewed Cartouche, whatever System said. A renewal keeps Increased Memory Limit.
- LocalDevVPN connects by itself: Renew now, Cartouche's own renewal, an update and the sign-in open LocalDevVPN, which connects and brings you back to Cartouche a second later. You no longer connect it first, nor leave it on all day. The first time, iOS asks you to allow LocalDevVPN's VPN configuration. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Renew every night: its steps in System now add LocalDevVPN's Enable LocalDevVPN action before Cartouche's Refresh All Apps, which waits for LocalDevVPN to connect. If you made this automation before, add that action before Refresh All Apps, or keep LocalDevVPN on.
- Renewal: an update or a sign-in started during a renewal waits for it to end, instead of failing with "Another refresh task is in progress". Renew now waits until Cartouche is set up.
- Continue on your iPhone a game you play on your Switch: export its save with JKSV or Checkpoint, put the folder or the ZIP in Files, then in the game's sheet choose Saves › Switch saves › Choose the exported save. Cartouche checks that it is this game's save and keeps the one it replaces, which you can put back. It works before the game's first start too.
- The other way, Make the ZIP for JKSV gives a ZIP of the game's save that JKSV restores on your Switch. Not yet tried with a real Switch save.
- Start a game with Siri or from your Home Screen: say "Play Mario Kart World in Cartouche", or use Cartouche's Play a Game action in the Shortcuts app and add that shortcut to your Home Screen as the game's own icon. A game's sheet shows how, under Siri and Home Screen. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- On an iPhone, games nxnative built since November 4 now turn upright as you hold your iPhone, their picture above their controls, as on the iPhone Duo's inner display: behind the console, and when they start in Cartouche's place. While the picture is on a TV, the iPhone stays sideways. Games built before stay sideways. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- The Home button over a game fades away a few seconds after the game shows: touch its place at the bottom of the screen to bring it back, then touch it again for the console.
- Games behind the console keep their full memory after Cartouche updates itself: an update no longer signs the games' process without Increased Memory Limit, which left some games about 3 GB there. A copy that already lost it gets it back at the update after this one, or at once by installing Cartouche again with the Cartouche Installer.
- Breath of the Wild, and any game that bundles a Metal pipeline archive for Apple's GPU, now installs signed whole: Cartouche signs only the files iOS loads code from. A game that still did not sign whole, or that stopped on an error as it started, now says why, with Sign again and play when that may help, instead of closing with nothing said. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Share a game's log from its sheet, under Share the log: Send the log… gives it as the game wrote it, zipped with a short summary, by Messages, Mail or AirDrop to whoever helps you; Send to Cartouche sends its start and its end to Cartouche privately, cleaned of the paths of your folders, addresses, names and identifiers, and What Cartouche receives shows exactly what goes first. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Testers' results: on a game's sheet, they now show in your language. Cartouche writes the measured ones itself, with your numbers and dates, and your iPhone translates what testers wrote, once, from iOS 18, with Show the original beside it.
- A game's description, once your iPhone has translated it, shows translated at once the next times you open its sheet, rather than after a few seconds each time.
- Mac: Cartouche Builder 0.6.0 builds each game for your Mac too and plays it there with your iPhone's saves, and its window shows Cartouche's console. Cartouche Builder 0.5.0 and older do not find this version by themselves: download it once from Cartouche's site, and it updates itself again after that.
- Mac: with your Mac paired, a game's sheet now shows its build on that Mac (building, waiting its turn, built, or why it failed), with a button that asks the Mac to build it, as you would on the Mac, and one that stops its build or takes it out of the queue.
- Mac: System has a new page for your Mac's builds: the game building and the queue, Build what is due and Stop all, every game of the Mac, which it follows or not, the builds that failed and why, and its nightly build. The Mac needs the newest version of its app; until then, Cartouche says to update it.
- Saves: Cartouche finds a game's folder in your saves storage by the game's ID, whatever name the Mac gave it, so the saves of a game played on your Mac come back to your iPhone.
- TV mode: if the TV only mirrors your iPhone, the game hides from it, so the touch controls never show there.
- TV mode: while your iPhone's screen is recorded, or shown on a Mac with iPhone Mirroring, the game hides the same way, with a button to show it anyway.
- On an iPad, a game made to be played sideways now stays sideways behind the console instead of turning upright with the iPad, and an iPad held upright asks you to turn it. Games nxnative built since November 4 turn with the iPad. Cartouche also calls your iPad an iPad rather than an iPhone. Never tried on a real iPad yet.
- Signing in to Apple is more reliable: when Apple's sign-in server answers "too many requests", Cartouche now sends the request again, a second apart, up to 10 times, as the Cartouche Installer does, instead of stopping and making you wait 15 minutes.
- When signing in stops because Cartouche could not reach your iPhone through LocalDevVPN, it now says what to do from why: put the pairing file in Cartouche again with the Cartouche Installer, first deleting the pairing the Installer keeps if your iPhone refused it, or restart your iPhone if it did not answer.
- If the built-in SideStore closes while you sign in, Cartouche now says so in your language, with what to do: restart your iPhone, then try again, and install the iOS update offered, if there is one. Until now it showed an English sentence, or the sign-in never ended.
- Put the pairing file in Cartouche again, in the Cartouche Installer, now does what it says: Cartouche uses the new pairing file. Until now it went on with the first one, so replacing a pairing file your iPhone refused did not help. The same goes for a pairing file you give Cartouche yourself.
- If your Cartouche never managed to sign in to Apple, install this version from a computer with the Cartouche Installer, over your copy: your games and saves stay. From now on, System says so itself in a copy that is not set up yet.
- Install with no computer, with your own signing certificate: each release now has a second IPA for a signing app on the iPhone, Cartouche-0.13.0-Own-Certificate.ipa for this one. Sign it there with your signing certificate (.p12) and its profile, open Cartouche and give it that certificate and its password: no Apple ID, no LocalDevVPN, no weekly renewal. Games start in Cartouche's place. System > Setup says whether the signature lacks Increased Memory Limit (about 3 GB without it) or the games' keychain groups, and each new version is offered to download, then sign again. Never tried with a real certificate yet.
- Signing apps: when one on the iPhone signed the IPA made for an Apple ID, signing in cannot work. Cartouche now says so as it opens, and offers the same version's IPA for your own certificate to sign instead, or the Cartouche Installer on a computer. Never tried with a signing app yet.
- Storage: when an SMB server does not sign you in, Cartouche now says why in its own words, as it opens a folder and as Add a shared folder lists the shared folders: the user name or the password refused, a server that does not let guests in, an account locked after too many wrong passwords, or one the server holds back, with what to do, instead of "Logon Failure" or "Unknown error".
- New at the end of System: Reset Cartouche erases what you put into Cartouche, your games with their saves and settings, the storage places you added, the Apple ID you signed in with and Cartouche's settings, then starts it again as just installed, without a computer. The signing certificate and the pairing file stay, so you only sign in again; your storage folders and what they hold stay too.
- Before it erases, Reset Cartouche names the games whose saves are not in your saves folder yet, and can send them there first.
- What's new: after each update, Cartouche now opens on what the new version brings, in your language when its notes are written in it, until you press Continue. System > What's new keeps the notes of the last ten versions.
- Measurements: Cartouche now also sends what renewed it and whether it opened LocalDevVPN first, how many times a sign-in asked Apple again, why it could not reach your iPhone, why a game did not sign whole or start, whether the signature of your own certificate carries Increased Memory Limit and the games' keychain groups, a game started by Siri or from the Home Screen, the memory limit a game in Cartouche's place had at its peak, and a start of Cartouche that never reached the console. Still never your Apple ID, your iPhone's name, your files or the addresses of your storage.
- Cartouche Installer 0.2.2, for Mac and Windows: its second step opens on the choice between your Apple Account and your own certificate, side by side, and Cartouche's IPA for your own certificate is refused with an Apple ID and installed with your own certificate.
