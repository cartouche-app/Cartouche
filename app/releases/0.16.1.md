Cartouche 0.16.1 installs again from your own SideStore: it asks your SideStore for the pairing file, and a copy without its app group says what to do.

- Your own SideStore: in 0.16.0, Cartouche asked its own built-in SideStore for the pairing file instead of yours; it asks yours again. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Without its app group: a copy of the IPA for SideStore installed without its app group, as some signing apps do, no longer asks for your Apple ID only to fail; it says so, and offers the IPA for your own certificate or the Installer instead. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
