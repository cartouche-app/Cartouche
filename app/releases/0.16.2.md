Cartouche 0.16.2 can bring a game of your NAS through your Mac and its cable, when the Mac is on Ethernet.

- NAS through your Mac: with the iPhone plugged into your Mac by a cable, and the Mac on Ethernet, the Mac reads the game on the NAS and passes it on through the cable, instead of the iPhone's Wi-Fi, and the install screen says By cable, through the Mac. On the Mac's Wi-Fi, the iPhone reads the NAS itself, as before. Needs Cartouche 0.8.1 or later on your Mac, which updates itself. Not tried with a real Mac and NAS yet.
