Cartouche 0.16.0 speaks Spanish and German, installs from your own SideStore without a computer, guides you through the nightly renewal, reads several games folders, reaches your Mac by cable and sends your saves while you play.

- Spanish and German: Cartouche now speaks Spanish and German besides English and French, on the iPhone, the iPad and the Mac, and follows your device's language as before. The guides stay in English and French. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Your own SideStore: you can now install Cartouche with the SideStore you already have, no computer needed. Add the source source.cartouche.cloud to your SideStore and install Cartouche from it, keeping its extensions on the main profile. Its setup asks your SideStore for the pairing file, takes its certificate when you sign in with the same Apple ID, then reinstalls Cartouche once to get Increased Memory Limit. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Renew every night: the setup's last step guides you through the Shortcuts automation that renews Cartouche every night, and Cartouche now sees each of its runs: the step and System say whether it is to make, on, failed (no LocalDevVPN, interrupted, or SideStore's error) or no longer running. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Expired Apple session: when Apple says the session Cartouche renews with has expired, Cartouche signs in again by itself once and renews itself; otherwise the System page asks you to sign in again, and Cartouche no longer retries every six hours. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Your first game: with no game yet, How to get a game, beside Import my copy on the home and on a game's sheet, opens the guide. With no Mac, its third step now offers Cartouche Cloud, which builds your game for you, and I have my own IPAs already, which opens Files; linked to Cartouche Cloud, its last step installs the game it built, and the guide opens on the step left. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Several games folders: in Storage, Add another games folder reads another folder too, on any of your servers. A game in several shows once and installs from a folder whose server answers, so a game on your NAS that your Google Drive also holds installs away from home; its sheet says where the other copies are, and an install whose server does not answer goes on from the next copy. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Folders from the Mac: the games folders and the saves folder can now be changed on your Mac's console too, on one of your connections, and your iPhone takes them the next time Cartouche opens, the folder chosen last winning on either side. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Mac by cable: plugged into your Mac with a cable, the iPhone reaches Cartouche on your Mac and installs your games from it without Wi-Fi; Wi-Fi takes over again once unplugged. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Install speed: while a game installs from your Mac or a NAS, its screen shows how it comes (cable, Tailscale, or the Mac's Wi-Fi band) and how fast, with a tip when the Mac is on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or the game comes slowly. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Saves sent while playing: a game behind the console sends its saves to your saves folder a few seconds after each save, not only after the game; offline, they still go after it. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- iCloud backup: the games Cartouche installs no longer go into your iPhone's backup, iCloud's or a computer's, as they install again from your storage; their saves still do. Games already installed leave it the first time this version opens. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Other installed apps: an IPA you install that is not an nxnative game is now on the System page, under Other installed apps, to open or uninstall; Cartouche no longer sends you to an Apps tab that does not exist. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- iPhones without BC: on an iPhone whose GPU reads no BC textures (iPhone 11 to 15), System and the question before an install now say that Cartouche on your Mac builds its games from nxnative-nobc, a separate fork of nxnative that converts those textures, once the two are paired. A game drawn with Vulkan whose build converts them is no longer Not supported on this iPhone, and a converted game still on your Mac or in a games folder no longer shows the warning before its install: its sheet says Textures converted for this GPU by this game's build. Games drawn with OpenGL keep the warning. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Game sheet memory: with Keep Cartouche open behind the game off, a game's sheet now counts the 8 GB Game Mode gives Cartouche a moment after it opens, on iPhones with 12 GB. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Cartouche Cloud games: a game you built and already installed now says On this iPhone instead of Install, and the Cloud page shows each game's cover and the four phases of its build as it runs. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Cartouche on the Mac: the Mac's app is now called Cartouche, as on the iPhone, and the Storage page says Add your Mac.
