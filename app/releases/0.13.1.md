Cartouche 0.13.1 brings the fixes made since 0.13.0, and its update gives the games behind the console their full memory back.

- Memory: updated from 0.13.0 inside Cartouche, a copy that had lost Increased Memory Limit at an earlier update, about 3 GB for a game behind the console, gets it back, as 0.13.0 no longer signs the games' process without it. Games then start behind the console again, with the Home button, but for those that need more, such as Mario Kart World, which start in Cartouche's place. From 0.12.1 or older, the update after this one brings it back. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Renewal: when Apple revoked the certificate Cartouche is signed with, most likely because another app or computer took a new one with the same Apple ID, Cartouche now says to install it again over itself with the Cartouche Installer, on your computer, with the same Apple ID, which keeps your games and saves. Until then it no longer renews itself on that signature, and System says so under Renew now. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- When a renewal or an update could not reach your iPhone, Cartouche now says what to do, as the sign-in does: connect LocalDevVPN, join a Wi-Fi network, or put the pairing file in Cartouche again. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Reports, and the game logs you send to Cartouche, keep their Storage line whole when your user name or a name in your storage settings is one of its words, such as games: that word is no longer taken out as if it were yours.
