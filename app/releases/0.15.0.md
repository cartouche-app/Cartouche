Cartouche now installs the games Cartouche Cloud builds for you without a Mac, no longer says Not supported on this iPhone of a game whose build converts its textures for your iPhone's GPU, and says in its own words what Apple answers when a sign-in does not go through.

- Cartouche Cloud: no Mac? Cartouche Cloud now builds your games for you, from the copy you send it on its site. In System › Cartouche Cloud, link Cartouche to your account with a code, follow each build, then install the games built, as from your games folder. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Game sheets: four more games have theirs, made from what players measured: Final Fantasy Resonance, Link's Awakening, Mario vs. Donkey Kong and Pokopia, which now shows On iPhone. The other sheets count the sessions played since. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Game sheets: on an iPhone whose GPU reads no BC textures, with an A16 chip or older, a game whose build converts them to ASTC, as a fork of nxnative does, is no longer Not supported on this iPhone: its sheet says Textures converted to ASTC by this game's build, and how it fits is decided as on other iPhones, by iOS, Increased Memory Limit, its memory and its results. A build that does not convert them, and a game drawn with Vulkan or OpenGL, keep the warning. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Signing in: when Apple refuses for now to set up a new sign-in on your iPhone, Cartouche now asks it once instead of once for each anisette server, says so in your language and has you wait before trying again, as after Apple's limits. When Apple says the sign-in has expired (1100), or refuses it without saying why (-22405), Cartouche now says what to do in your language too. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- TV mode: when the TV shows only the game's card, Report a problem now says whether the game's picture was there, and which nxnative built the game. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
