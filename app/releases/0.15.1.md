Cartouche 0.15.1 restores Apple Account sign-in.

- Apple sign-in: Cartouche uses the client format Apple expects for new sign-ins. This fixes the HTTP 503 at the start of sign-in.
- Update by computer: if you couldn’t sign in to update Cartouche, install this version over your current copy with Cartouche Installer 0.2.3. Your games and saves stay on the iPhone.
- Game sheets: a game’s sheet shows results for your device model first, with the newest results first in each group. The genre gets its own line when space is tight.
