Cartouche 0.17.0 adds mods for each game, keeps your music playing, pauses a game when its controller disconnects, lets your Mac renew Cartouche and carries every profile of a game with its saves.

- Mods: on a game's sheet, Mods adds one from Files, as its folder or its ZIP, turns each on or off and sets their order, the one higher up winning. A mod that changes the game's code too is left out, with why. On your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Home filter: with games installed and games not installed yet, on your Mac, in a games folder or in Cartouche Cloud, a menu beside My library shows All, Installed or Not installed, and L and R change it with a controller. Cartouche keeps your choice. Also on your Mac. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Saves folder: once your first game is over, while its saves are on this iPhone alone, the home offers to keep them safe in a folder on iCloud Drive, your Mac or a NAS: a tap opens Storage, and its × takes it off for good. A reinstall then loses nothing. Also on your Mac. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Your music while playing: turn on Keep other apps’ sound, in System › While a game runs, and the music or podcast you play in another app goes on under the game's sound, from the game's next start. Off until you turn it on. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Controller lost: a game pauses when its last controller disconnects. 3 seconds later the console comes back and says so, and connecting a controller again brings the game back. A game you play on the screen alone never pauses; System › While a game runs turns it off. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Renewed by your Mac: signed in once on the iPhone page of Cartouche for Mac, your Mac renews Cartouche on your iPhone in its last two days, by the cable or on your Wi-Fi, with no reinstall, and Cartouche shows its new date. Your password goes to Apple alone, and is asked again only once Apple ends the session. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Reinstalled by your Mac: when Cartouche's certificate is gone from your Apple Account, your Mac installs the newest Cartouche over it, with its games and saves: by itself once it expires, or at once with Reinstall on the iPhone page of Cartouche for Mac. It never revokes a certificate. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Every profile and shader: the saves of a game's players 2 to 8 now go and come with the first's, on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, each with the room the game made for it. A save from JKSV keeps the room it had on your console. And the shaders a game prepared come back after a reinstall, a reset or on another device, with those your other devices prepared, so its first games stutter less. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Back to the start: Put back a copy, on a game's sheet, now also lists the copies the game keeps on this iPhone of the saves each session starts from, even without a saves folder. If a game or a mod went wrong, put back the copy of its start; the saves it replaces are kept too. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Settings in your language: every setting nxnative gives a game now reads in your language, Rotation, Texture filtering and Microphone among them. Rotation now locks a game sideways or upright behind Cartouche's console too, as soon as you pick it in the game's menu. A game that has Polish or Thai starts in it on an iPhone set to it. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Prompts in your language: the prompt that waits for more players and the four labels of nxnative's menu over a game now read in Cartouche's language on your iPhone too, as on the Mac, behind the console or in Cartouche's place. The settings in that menu keep nxnative's English names. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Out of room: when a game stops because your iPhone ran out of room for the memory it keeps in files while it plays, Cartouche now says so and how much was free, instead of only saying the game closed. Before a start that may end that way, it warns you, with Play anyway. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Pipelines for your GPU: Cartouche now tells your Mac your iPhone's GPU, so that a game that bundles its Metal 4 pipelines, such as Breath of the Wild, is built with them for your iPhone too and no longer prepares them for minutes at its first start. Needs Cartouche for Mac 0.9.0, and a new build of the game. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Converted builds: on an iPhone whose GPU reads no BC textures, the question before an install now reads the game's build first: a build that converts its textures, from your Mac, a games folder or Cartouche Cloud, installs at once, and a Cartouche Cloud game built from nxnative-nobc says so on its sheet. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- iPhone 11 to 15: on an iPhone whose GPU reads no BC textures, the question before an install and System now say that Cartouche Cloud builds its games from nxnative-nobc too, for a game you send for an iPhone 11 to 15. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- LocalDevVPN port: signing in, renewing and updating no longer stop at once when another service of your iPhone answers at the port Cartouche reaches it at through LocalDevVPN: Cartouche looks for the right port again. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Certificate import: Import signing certificate opens the same Files picker as Import my copy, for the certificate (.p12) and its profile, so that the certificate you tap there comes back to the page, on iOS 27.3 too. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Install screen: while a game installs from your Mac on Wi-Fi, its screen no longer says cable because your iPhone was plugged in to the Mac for something else.
