Cartouche now reaches your Mac and NAS away from home with Tailscale, your iPhone and your Mac share their storage connections and folders, and each copy of a game's saves names the version of the game that wrote it.

- Tailscale: installed apart on this iPhone and on your Mac or NAS, it lets Cartouche reach them away from home, your Mac by itself and a NAS by its name or address in Tailscale, and a Mac never met on your Wi-Fi pairs under Add your Mac › By its address. Storage › Away from home says whether it is connected. While it is on, Cartouche renews itself alone only in its last 2 days, as LocalDevVPN turns Tailscale off, and tells you when to turn it on again. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Game sheets: eight more games have theirs, made from what players measured: Super Mario Bros. Wonder, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, Pokémon Legends: Z-A, Ocarina of Time, Donkey Kong Bananza and Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave. Their page says whether your iPhone has what the game needs, and how it ran on iPhones like yours, and Cartouche starts each game where its memory fits. A game's title keeps its lines and its size on its page. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Storage: the Mac you pair with Cartouche Builder now keeps the same connections as this iPhone, passwords included, through the bridge's encryption: one you add, change or remove on either comes to the other. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Mac: when the saves folder or the games folder you chose is on one of your connections, your Mac with Cartouche Builder now keeps its games' saves in that saves folder, and sends its games to that games folder. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- Mac: Cartouche Builder 0.7.0, which updates itself from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1, builds Mario Kart World again and gives Cartouche's console on your Mac a game's settings and saves on its sheet, and the System page as on your iPhone, with Your first game, Storage, Game measurements and Report a problem. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- Builds: each build on your Mac now says what it is for, the iPhone, the Mac or both, on the game's sheet and in System › Builds, and a game whose app for the Mac alone is left to build says so, with Build its app for the Mac. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Saves: each copy of a game's saves now names the version of the game that wrote it, and an older version of the game never takes it: the game's sheet says the copy comes once the game is updated, and the saves played meanwhile go to your saves folder as a copy of their own. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Storage: How to name your games, under the games folder, shows with made-up examples what Cartouche finds in your games folder and your saves folder, and what Cartouche Builder finds in its dumps folder, each name found or left aside, and why. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Controllers: a controller now plays the game you start with Play. A game started that way got nothing from the controller, though it drove the console and hid the game's touch controls. Not tried on a real iPhone or iPad yet.
- After you free room by uninstalling games, an install is no longer refused with the room your iPhone had before, as Cartouche now reads the room again for each install.
- With your own certificate, when the certificate you choose does not go in, Cartouche now says why, such as a profile or a .zip chosen in its place, a password that does not open it, a .p12 without its private key, or, signed on your iPhone, a Cartouche to sign again in your signing app. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- When the part of Cartouche the built-in SideStore runs in (LiveProcess) is missing from your copy, a sign-in, a renewal or an update now says to install Cartouche again over itself with the Cartouche Installer, on your computer, with the same Apple ID, which puts it back and keeps your games and saves, instead of a message that sent you to other tools. Until then Cartouche no longer renews itself, and System says so under Renew now. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Reset Cartouche now empties the folder iOS keeps for what other apps open Cartouche with, instead of stopping on it, and goes through to the end. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- Your first game: the link it sends your Mac now opens Cartouche Builder's page at cartouche.cloud, Cartouche's own address.
- Reports now also keep whole the From and To lines of an install from your games folder or a copy to it, and the journal's lines on saves and on Reset Cartouche, when your user name or a name in your storage settings is one of their words, such as games.
- Game settings: Press a button to join, Match screen for the picture shape, and the new note under Dynamic resolution now show in French too.
