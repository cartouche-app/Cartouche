## Your games, saves and accounts

Cartouche sends your games and saves nowhere but the storage you set up yourself, with your own account. Passwords stay in your iPhone's keychain, and go to your paired Mac alone, encrypted. Your Apple account goes only to Apple, to sign Cartouche and your games. To renew Cartouche from your Mac, Cartouche for Mac sends your password to Apple alone, and keeps only the session Apple gives in return, readable by your Mac account only, never the password. Cartouche downloads no game but those Cartouche Cloud built for the account you linked it to.

## Game measurements

After each game, Cartouche sends what nxnative measured while it ran, so that games run better on iPhone, iPad and Mac:

- **For each game session:** frame rate, frame and graphics times, stutters, shader preparation, memory, heat and power, how many mods were on, and how the session ended.
- **About your device:** its model, iOS version, language and memory limit, Cartouche's version, and a random number of this copy of Cartouche.
- **Cartouche's own events:** starts, installs, updates, renewals and sign-ins, as counts and results.

Never your Apple account, your iPhone's name, your files, the addresses of your storage or a game's log.

**System › Game measurements** shows what is sent and when, turns it off, and **Erase what this iPhone sent** deletes every report of this copy of Cartouche. On the Mac, the console's System page has the same.

Reports are deleted after 25 months. They are published only as figures of at least three iPhones, or three Macs, each week: in the games' sheets, and for nxnative's developers, so that they see which games run well where.

## Reports and logs

A report or a log leaves your device only when you send it, as you choose. Sent to Cartouche, it goes without passwords, server names or addresses, user names or Apple account.

## Cartouche Cloud

Your copy is deleted as soon as the game is built; the game built stays two days, for your account alone. Your keys never leave your computer. Its own [privacy page](https://build.cartouche.cloud/privacy) has the details.
