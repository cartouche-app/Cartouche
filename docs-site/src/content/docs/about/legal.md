Cartouche contains no games, no keys and no firmware, and downloads only the games Cartouche Cloud built for you, from the copy you sent it yourself. It has no catalogue of games to download, no link to one and no public storage. You import games you own and built yourself, or that Cartouche Cloud built for you.

- **No games or game updates**, in any form.
- **No keys or firmware.**
- **No download links**, in the app, on its sites or in this documentation.

Use only games you own, dumped from your own console with your own keys, as the law of your country allows. Cartouche for Mac builds them on your Mac, from your own files, into your own games folder.

Messages, issues and pull requests that contain or link to games, keys, firmware or IPAs built from games are removed, on GitHub as on Discord.
