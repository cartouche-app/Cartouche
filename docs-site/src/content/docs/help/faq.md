## Is Cartouche free?

Yes. It is free software under the AGPL-3.0, and it works with a free Apple account. Only Cartouche Cloud, which builds your games if you have no Mac, is paid, per build that succeeds.

## Do I need a computer?

Not with SideStore: [add Cartouche's source to it](/start/sidestore/). Otherwise once, to install Cartouche from a Mac or a Windows PC. Then it renews and updates itself from the iPhone or iPad. Building your games takes a Mac with Apple silicon, with Cartouche for Mac, or a computer and Cartouche Cloud.

## Which games run?

The ones nxnative runs natively. In Cartouche, **All games** lists every game that has a profile in nxnative, and its sheet says whether it runs on your iPhone or iPad; the **On Mac** filter shows those nxnative runs on the Mac.

## Does Cartouche come with these games?

No. Cartouche provides no game, no key and no firmware: you build your own from your own copies. It shows their official covers, which come from IGDB. See [No games included](/about/legal/).

## How do I build my games?

With [Cartouche for Mac](/mac/install/), on a Mac with Apple silicon: show it the folder of your copies and your games folder, and it installs what it needs, builds each game with nxnative, then builds it again when nxnative improves it. With no Mac, [Cartouche Cloud](/cloud/) builds them for you.

## Can I add a game's updates and DLC?

Yes. With Cartouche for Mac, put them in your dumps folder with the game: the Builder builds them into it, and builds the game again when you add a newer one. With Cartouche Cloud, send them with the base game. See [Updates and DLC](/mac/builder/#updates-and-dlc) on the Mac, and [on Cartouche Cloud](/cloud/#updates-and-dlc).

## Does Cartouche work on iPad and on a Mac?

On an iPad with an M1 chip or newer, it is the same app as on the iPhone ([On iPad](/iphone/ipad/)). On a Mac with Apple silicon, Cartouche for Mac builds each of your games for the Mac too and plays it there, with the same saves and the same connections as your iPhone and iPad.

## Do my games count against a free account's three apps?

No. Your games run inside Cartouche, which is the one app of yours they need.

## Can I play on my TV?

Yes: connect the iPhone to the TV with a cable, or mirror its screen with AirPlay. The TV shows the game alone, and the controls stay on the iPhone. There is no Apple TV version.

## Which controllers work?

The controllers iOS supports, over Bluetooth or by cable: every screen of Cartouche, and your games, take them. Touch works everywhere too.

## Can I use my own signing certificate?

Yes, from a computer, with the Cartouche Installer: see [Your own certificate](/installer/own-certificate/). A copy of Cartouche signed by another app on the iPhone starts no game, except the one from Cartouche's source for SideStore.

## What does Cartouche Cloud keep of my games?

Your copy, while it is built: it is deleted as soon as the game is built, and two days after you sent it at the latest. The game built stays two days, for your account alone. Your keys never reach it. See [Cartouche Cloud](/cloud/#what-it-keeps).

## Does Cartouche send any data?

Measurements of your game sessions, such as frame rate and memory, so that games run better; never your files, saves or accounts. You can turn them off in **System › Game measurements**, on the iPhone as on the Mac. See [Privacy](/about/privacy/).

## Which languages does Cartouche speak?

English, French, Spanish and German: Cartouche follows the language of your iPhone, iPad or Mac, as the Installer does. So does this documentation.
