## Discord

[Cartouche's Discord server](https://discord.gg/wG3DZjdAeF) has help for the iPhone, the iPad and the Mac, the news of each version, and corners in French, Spanish and German.

## Give them what they need

- **Copy the report**, under each error, copies what it takes to understand it, without your passwords or addresses. Paste it in your message.
- **System › Report a problem** shows a full report: what you did, and Cartouche's last steps and errors. **Send to Cartouche** sends it to the project, privately; or send it by Messages, Mail or AirDrop to whoever helps you.
- **Share the log**, on a game's sheet, shares what the game wrote as it ran. **Send to Cartouche** removes your paths, addresses and names first, and shows you what goes.

On the Mac, the console's **System › Report a problem** and a game's **Share the log** do the same.

## A bug?

A bug can also go to GitHub, as an [issue](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche/issues/new/choose). Issues are public: check what you paste.

:::caution
Never post a game, a key, firmware or a link to one, on Discord or GitHub: such messages are removed.
:::
