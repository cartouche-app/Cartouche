Cartouche for Mac 0.9.0 renews Cartouche on your iPhone, installs it again over itself when its certificate is gone, adds mods for each game, pauses a game when its controller disconnects, and keeps every profile of a game with its saves.

- **Cartouche renewed from this Mac.** Under Renewing Cartouche, on the iPhone page, sign in once with your Apple Account: this Mac then renews Cartouche on the iPhones it reaches, by cable or on Wi-Fi, in its last two days, with no reinstall, and Renew does it at once. The password goes to Apple alone and is never kept; a notification asks for it again only once Apple ended the session. Not tried on a real Mac and iPhone yet.
- **Cartouche installed again over itself.** When Cartouche's certificate is gone from your Apple Account, this Mac installs the newest Cartouche over it, from its releases on GitHub, signed for the same team: its games and saves stay. It does it by itself once Cartouche expired, and Reinstall, on the iPhone page, does it at once, showing how far it is. It never revokes a certificate. Not tried on a real Mac and iPhone yet.
- **Mods for each game.** A game's sheet in the console has Mods, as on the iPhone: add one from a folder or a ZIP on this Mac, turn it on or off and set the order; the game reads them at its next start. Mods that change the game's code are not supported on the Mac yet. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **A lost controller pauses the game.** When the last controller disconnects during a game, the console comes back in front 3 seconds later, the game paused, and a controller connected again brings it back. System › While a game runs turns it off. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Every profile, and the shaders.** The console keeps the saves of a game's profiles 2 to 8 in step with the iPhone's, with the first's and the room the game made for each, and the shaders the game prepared on this Mac go beside its copies, where your iPhones' come from before it starts. Needs Cartouche 0.17.0 on your iPhones for a game with other profiles or larger saves. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Back to the start of a game.** In the console, Put back a copy on a game's sheet also lists the copies the game keeps on this Mac of the saves each session starts from, and puts one back the way it puts back a copy of your saves folder, the saves it replaces kept. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Pipelines for your iPhone's GPU.** A game that bundles its Metal 4 pipelines, such as Breath of the Wild, now has them made for the GPU of each paired iPhone too, not only the iPhone 17's, so its first start no longer builds them for minutes. Needs Cartouche 0.17.0 on your iPhones, which tells this Mac their GPU. A game built before gets it at its next build. Not tried on a real iPhone yet.
- **DLC images beside a .nup.** A .nup that holds no DLC of its own is built with the images of its DLC you put in a folder named aoc beside it: 1.img, 2.img… by the DLC's number, as nxnative makes them. A new image builds the game again; what is left out, the library says. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **nxnative's newest settings in your language.** Texture filtering, Microphone and Microphone gain, and the Mac's picture shapes read in your language in the console, and a game that has Polish or Thai starts in it on a Mac set to it. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Out of room on the disk.** A game that nxnative stops because this Mac's disk ran out of room for the memory it keeps in files now says so, and how much was left, instead of only that it closed. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **A filter on the home.** With games installed on this Mac and others still waiting in your games folders, a menu beside My library shows All, Installed or Not installed, as on the iPhone, and Cartouche keeps your choice. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **A saves folder offered.** Back on the console after a game, while this Mac keeps the saves in the folder it took by itself, the home offers a saves folder of your own: a tap opens Storage, and its × takes it off for good. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **No more half an IPA after a slow copy.** When putting a game's IPA in its folder on the same disk took more than 10 minutes, or failed, the build stopped at the IPA's step with "File exists"; the builder now removes what was left and copies it whole.
- **From 0.8.2, 0.8.1, 0.8.0, 0.7.1, 0.7.0 or 0.6.x**, which update themselves to 0.9.0: your settings, your games and their builds stay; from 0.7.1 or older, the app takes its new name, Cartouche, at its first start.
- **From 0.5.0 or older**, which do not find this version by themselves since Cartouche's repository moved to its own organization on GitHub: download it once, and it updates itself again after that. Your settings, your games and their builds stay.

Download `Cartouche-Builder-0.9.0.dmg`, for a Mac with Apple silicon (nxnative builds on macOS 26.6 or later), open it and drag **Cartouche** onto **Applications**. The app is not notarized: the first time, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. Its updates then open without asking.

Your games are yours: the builder builds the dumps of your own Switch, and shares nothing. How it works: [builder/README.md](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche/blob/main/builder/README.md).
