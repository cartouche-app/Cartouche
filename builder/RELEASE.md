Cartouche for Mac 0.8.0 is called Cartouche, as on the iPhone, builds for the iPhones you pair or a model you choose, from nxnative-nobc for one that reads no BC textures, reaches your iPhones by cable, changes their folders from its console, sends your saves while you play and speaks Spanish and German.

- **Cartouche, as on the iPhone.** Cartouche Builder is now called Cartouche, in the Finder, the Dock, the menu bar, its window and its disk image. A copy already installed updates itself as before, then takes the new name at its first start and opens again as it was, with its login item; its folder and its builds stay the same. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Build for.** Settings › Builds › Build for: the paired iPhones, by default, or a model chosen in a list. When one of them reads no BC textures (iPhone 11 to 15), the games are built from nxnative-nobc, a separate fork of nxnative by mianmuhammadse that converts them, and built again when that changes; otherwise from nxnative, as before. The setup assistant's tools step says which repository it downloads, and Change… picks another one before the download. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **iPhones by cable.** With Share with Cartouche on the iPhone on, an iPhone plugged into this Mac by a cable reaches it through the cable, no Wi-Fi needed, through macOS's own USB service: nothing to install; the iPhone page says an iPhone connected by cable. Not tried with a real iPhone yet.
- **How fast a game goes to the iPhone.** The iPhone page shows how fast the game an iPhone installs comes and how: by cable, Tailscale, or this Mac's Wi-Fi and its band, with a tip when this Mac is on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or it goes slowly; Cartouche on the iPhone shows the same on its install screen. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Folders chosen on the Mac.** On the console's Storage page, Change folder, Add another games folder, Stop reading this folder and Choose a folder change your iPhones' games folders and saves folder, on one of your connections: Cartouche uses them at once, and your iPhones take them the next time Cartouche opens on them, the folder chosen last winning. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Several games folders.** Cartouche's console lists the other games folders your iPhone reads, and offers the Mac apps another Mac sent to any of them, once each, from a folder that answers; builds still go to the first one. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Saves while playing.** A game played on this Mac sends its saves to the saves folder a few seconds after each save, from a copy made at one moment, one copy for each game, as Cartouche does on the iPhone. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Out of Time Machine.** This Mac's copy of a game your NAS keeps in one file is left out of Time Machine as it is installed or made again of a new build; the file on the NAS, and the game's saves, stay in your backups. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Older nxnative for a game's shaders.** When nxnative's newest commit splits a game's shaders otherwise than its profile says, the builder now goes back through every change of nxnative's splitter since that profile, not the three newest, and remembers what each try showed: a game this Mac built before builds again from the same commit, however often nxnative changes its splitter. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Spanish and German.** Cartouche on the Mac, its console and the builder's messages now speak Spanish and German too, following the Mac's language, and the settings offer the four languages. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **How to get a game.** In Cartouche's console, with no game yet, How to get a game, beside Import my copy, opens the console's guide.
- **From 0.7.1, 0.7.0 or 0.6.x**, which update themselves to 0.8.0: your settings, your games and their builds stay, and the app takes its new name at its first start.
- **From 0.5.0 or older**, which do not find this version by themselves since Cartouche's repository moved to its own organization on GitHub: download it once, and it updates itself again after that. Your settings, your games and their builds stay.

Download `Cartouche-Builder-0.8.0.dmg`, for a Mac with Apple silicon (nxnative builds on macOS 26.6 or later), open it and drag **Cartouche** onto **Applications**. The app is not notarized: the first time, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. Its updates then open without asking.

Your games are yours: the builder builds the dumps of your own Switch, and shares nothing. How it works: [builder/README.md](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche/blob/main/builder/README.md).
