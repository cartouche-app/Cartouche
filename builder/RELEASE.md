Cartouche Builder 0.7.0 gives Cartouche's console on the Mac a game's settings and saves and the iPhone's System page, keeps your iPhone's storage connections and folders, and lets your iPhone reach this Mac away from home with Tailscale.

- **A game's settings in the console.** **Game settings**, in Cartouche's console, opens the game's settings as on the iPhone, without starting it: each choice is kept for every start, from Cartouche, the Finder or the Dock, Display mode fills the screen unless you choose a window, and a game starts in this Mac's language when it has it, until you choose another. A game's sheet shows how long you played it on this Mac and whether it runs here, and **Edit the game's info** changes its name, genre, summary and cover. The console writes sizes, dates and the games' languages in the language you chose for it. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **A game's saves in the console.** A game's sheet in Cartouche's console shows its saves as on the iPhone: when they were last in step with your saves folder and what the last sync brought, a sync now, **Put back a copy**, the copies newest first, the one put back becoming the newest in your saves folder, which your iPhone takes next, and **Switch saves**, a JKSV backup brought in or this Mac's saves made into a ZIP for JKSV. A saves folder that is not there, a NAS's share not mounted, is said as on the iPhone, and a sync that failed says why, with **Copy the report**. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Each copy names the game's version.** A copy of a game's saves names the version of the game that wrote it, and an older version of the game never takes it: the game's sheet, in the console and in the builder's window, says the newest copy comes once the game is updated here, and this Mac's saves go to the saves folder as a copy of their own meanwhile. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Eight more game sheets.** In Cartouche's console, the pages of Super Mario Bros. Wonder, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, Pokémon Legends: Z-A, Ocarina of Time, Donkey Kong Bananza and Fire Emblem Fortune's Weave show their sheet: what the game needs of this Mac, and what players measured of it on their iPhones. A long title keeps its two lines there. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **The iPhone's System page in the console.** System, on the console's dock, now opens the iPhone's System page, with what a Mac has: **Your first game**, **Storage**, **Open Cartouche Builder**, **This Mac** (its model, chip, memory, macOS and BC textures), the builder's updates as its settings show them, **Game measurements**, the version and the game sheets, and **Report a problem**. Storage is the iPhone's page, with the same connections as the iPhones you paired, passwords included, kept in a file of the builder's folder that only you can read, and This Mac's folders. Game measurements now also sends what nxnative measured of each game played on this Mac, as Cartouche does on the iPhone, and turns it off with the builder's own switch. A game's sheet shares its log, and the console writes its dates in its own language. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **A guide to a first game.** **Your first game** takes you through this Mac's three steps, ticked by themselves in any order: the setup assistant, a game built for this Mac, a game played from the console. Its card stays at the top of the console's home until you play one there, or close it. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Your iPhone's folders, on your connections.** When the saves folder chosen on your iPhone is on one of your connections, a NAS's shared folder, an S3 bucket, an FTP or SFTP server or a Google Drive, the builder keeps the saves of the games played on this Mac there, through the same connection. When the games folder chosen there is, the builder sends it each IPA and each Mac app of its games folder, the newest first, never during a batch, and never removes anything there: the console's Storage page and the builder's iPhone page show how many went, the one going, or why none goes, with **Send now**. The Mac apps another Mac sent there install from the console's library. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Away from home, with Tailscale.** With Tailscale on this Mac and on the iPhone, signed in to the same account, the paired iPhones learn this Mac's address in Tailscale and reach it there away from home. The **iPhone** page says whether Tailscale is on, with that address, by which an iPhone never met on the Wi-Fi pairs, or links to Tailscale for Mac. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **Each build says what it is for.** Builds, a game's page, the menu bar and the queue say on a line of their own whether a build is for the iPhone, this Mac or both, and a game built or failed says what for. The iPhone says it too, and asks this Mac for a game's app for the Mac alone when only that app is due. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **How to name your games.** **Naming your games**, under **Folders** in the settings, in the setup assistant's steps of the dumps and of the games folder, and in the library when it finds no game or leaves files aside, shows with made-up examples which names the builder finds in your dumps folder and which Cartouche finds in your games folder and saves folder, each found or left aside, and why. Not tried on a real Mac yet.
- **The room on this Mac, read again.** When your iPhone copies a game to this Mac's games folder, the builder tells it the room the folder has now, not the room it read before, which could be out of date after you freed some.
- **The builder readable after the console.** On a Mac in light mode, the builder's titles and words no longer come back white on white after the console, the sidebar and the cards dark: the window is as it was before the console.
- **From 0.6.1 or 0.6.0**, which update themselves to 0.7.0: your settings, your games and their builds stay.
- **From 0.5.0 or older**, which do not find this version by themselves since Cartouche's repository moved to its own organization on GitHub: download it once, and it updates itself again after that. Your settings, your games and their builds stay.

Download `Cartouche-Builder-0.7.0.dmg`, for a Mac with Apple silicon (nxnative builds on macOS 26.6 or later), open it and drag **Cartouche Builder** onto **Applications**. The app is not notarized: the first time, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. Its updates then open without asking.

Your games are yours: the builder builds the dumps of your own Switch, and shares nothing. How it works: [builder/README.md](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche/blob/main/builder/README.md).
