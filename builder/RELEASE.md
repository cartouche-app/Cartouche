Cartouche for Mac 0.8.2 keeps your build folders on your NAS between two builds, and builds each game on this Mac.

- **Build folders on your NAS.** Build folders chosen on a NAS now keep the games' build folders there between two builds, never build there: each game is built on this Mac, its build folder brought back from the NAS first, with its shader caches and its app's build, then sent back once built, freeing this Mac. The game's extracted files never travel. Storage lists those folders apart, under Build folders on your NAS. Builds failed with the build folders on a NAS before. Not tried on a real Mac and NAS yet.
- **Games of the NAS for an iPhone by cable.** Cartouche 0.16.2 on the iPhone now asks for it: with an iPhone plugged into this Mac, and this Mac on Ethernet, Cartouche reads a game of the iPhone's games folders on your NAS (shared folder, FTP or SFTP) through your connection, and passes it on through the cable; on this Mac's Wi-Fi, the iPhone reads the NAS itself. Only the games folders, never a bucket nor a Google Drive. Not tried on a real Mac and NAS yet.
- **From 0.8.1, 0.8.0, 0.7.1, 0.7.0 or 0.6.x**, which update themselves to 0.8.2: your settings, your games and their builds stay; from 0.7.1 or older, the app takes its new name, Cartouche, at its first start.
- **From 0.5.0 or older**, which do not find this version by themselves since Cartouche's repository moved to its own organization on GitHub: download it once, and it updates itself again after that. Your settings, your games and their builds stay.

Download `Cartouche-Builder-0.8.2.dmg`, for a Mac with Apple silicon (nxnative builds on macOS 26.6 or later), open it and drag **Cartouche** onto **Applications**. The app is not notarized: the first time, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. Its updates then open without asking.

Your games are yours: the builder builds the dumps of your own Switch, and shares nothing. How it works: [builder/README.md](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche/blob/main/builder/README.md).
