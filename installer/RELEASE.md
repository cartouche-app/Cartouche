Cartouche Installer 0.2.4 speaks Spanish and German, and shows an iPhone plugged in while it is still looking for iPhones.

- Spanish and German: the installer now speaks Spanish and German too, following your computer's language, and offers them in its language menu. Not tried on a real computer yet.
- An iPhone plugged in while the installer is still looking for iPhones now shows up when you click Refresh; before, it only showed up once the installer was opened again. Not tried with a real iPhone yet.

## Downloads

- **Mac**, Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. `Cartouche-Installer-0.2.4-Mac.dmg`. The first time you open the app, use **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway** if macOS asks.
- **Windows** 10 or 11. `Cartouche-Installer-0.2.4-Windows.exe`. If SmartScreen stops it, choose **More info › Run anyway**. Windows needs **Apple Devices** from the Microsoft Store, or iTunes, to reach the iPhone.
