## Downloads

- **Mac**, Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later. `Cartouche-Installer-0.2.3-Mac.dmg`. The first time you open the app, use **System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway** if macOS asks.
- **Windows** 10 or 11. `Cartouche-Installer-0.2.3-Windows.exe`. If SmartScreen stops it, choose **More info › Run anyway**. Windows needs **Apple Devices** from the Microsoft Store, or iTunes, to reach the iPhone.
