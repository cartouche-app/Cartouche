Cartouche Installer 0.2.2 opens its second step on a clean choice between your Apple Account and your own certificate.

- **Apple Account or your own certificate.** The second step opens on the choice between the two, side by side, in place of a link-like button under Sign in; with a certificate, the step says so and its password has a field of its own.
- **Cartouche's IPA for your own certificate.** Each release of Cartouche now has a second IPA, for a signing app on the iPhone. The installer refuses that IPA with an Apple Account, and installs it with your own certificate.
- Installing again still updates Cartouche and keeps its games and saves.

Downloads:

- **Mac**, Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later: `Cartouche-Installer-0.2.2-Mac.dmg`. The app is not notarized: the first time, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows** 10 or 11: `Cartouche-Installer-0.2.2-Windows.exe`. If SmartScreen stops it, **More info › Run anyway**. Windows needs **Apple Devices** from the Microsoft Store, or iTunes, to reach the iPhone.
