# 175. The showcase page once in each language, each at its own address, for search engines

2026-11-26

The site publishes its page once in each of its four languages, each at its own address: English at the root, <https://cartouche.cloud/>, French, Spanish and German at `/fr/`, `/es/` and `/de/`. [`site/index.html`](../../site/index.html) still holds every text in the four, side by side, and shows the visitor's language; the [Showcase site](../../.github/workflows/site.yml) workflow writes the four pages from it ([`site/tools/languages.py`](../../site/tools/languages.py)), each with its own language's texts alone, its title, description and what a shared link shows in that language, its own address (`canonical`, `og:url`) and the three others (`hreflang`, English for any other language). The English page sends a visitor who reads French, Spanish or German, or chose one of them on the site, on to their page, at the same place; the language buttons lead to the page in the language chosen.

- **What a results page shows** names what players search for: Cartouche, the Switch games nxnative runs natively, iPhone, iPad and Mac, in each language. The site names Switch for what the games are, as the owner chose; it says Cartouche is affiliated with neither Nintendo nor anyone else, as it did. The first paragraph says *the Switch games nxnative runs natively*, and the questions start with *Is Cartouche an emulator?*
- **What search engines read of the app** (`application/ld+json`): the site, and Cartouche as a free game application for iOS, iPadOS and macOS, with its description in the page's language, its documentation, its repository and its licence. Never a price, an offer or a rating.
- **`robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`**: every page may be read; the sitemap lists the four pages, each with the three others, and the download page. The documentation has its own, which Starlight writes, and its `robots.txt` names it.
- **Google Search Console** knows the domain as the owner's: its TXT record is in [`domain/records.json`](../../domain/records.json) ([domain/README.md](../../domain/README.md#search-engines)).

It amends [decision 40](../decisions.md) and [decision 152](152.md), whose page was one address in every language.

**Why.** On November 26, 2026, the owner found Cartouche very hard to find on Google. The site had no `robots.txt` or sitemap, a search for its address found nothing, and its one page held its four languages at once, hidden from each other by its styles: a search engine reads a page by its address, in one language, and could neither tell its languages apart nor offer a French reader the French page. Its title, *Cartouche for iPhone, iPad and Mac*, named neither nxnative nor the games, which are what players type.
