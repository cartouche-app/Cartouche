# 127. Each build says what it is for: the iPhone, the Mac, or both

2026-11-17

A Mac builds a game for the iPhone, its IPA, for itself, its app, or for both, the app after the IPA in the same batch, or the app alone once the IPA is up to date ([decision 95](095.md)), and each build now says which, on both sides. The engine's status says what each game's build makes once the batch has planned it (`makes`: `"ios"`, then `"mac"`, or one of the two, [`status.py`](../../builder/cartouche_builder/status.py)), beside the part it makes now (`platform`), as it said already. The Mac's window says it as a line of its own above the step, which no longer begins with "iPhone ·" or "This Mac ·": **For the iPhone**, **For this Mac**, **For the iPhone and this Mac**, and, building both, **For the iPhone, then this Mac**, then **Done for the iPhone, now for this Mac**; in **Builds**, on the game's page and in the menu bar for the game building, and in the queue for each game waiting its turn, with what it will make: as the batch planned it, or, a game asked for before the plan, as the window asked for it and as the game's settings say, its app alone when only that app is due and the game was not asked again, both otherwise ([`Theme.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/CartoucheBuilder/Theme.swift), [`AppModel.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/CartoucheBuilder/AppModel.swift)). A game built says what for, **Built for the iPhone and this Mac**, and a game whose build failed, **Failed for the iPhone** or **Failed for this Mac**.

Over the bridge, each game says what the Mac builds it for (`platforms`), a game building or waiting what its build makes and, building, the one it makes now (`makes`, `now`), and a game built for both, its app's own last build and failure, and whether that app alone is due (`mac`); a game built for the Mac alone has the dates and the failure of its app, where they were its IPA's ([`CartoucheBridgeBuilds.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBridgeBuilds.swift), [`BridgeBuilds.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/BridgeBuilds.swift)). On the iPhone, a game's sheet says what its build is for, under its first line while it builds, as the page of the Mac's builds does, under its line otherwise: **For the iPhone and the Mac**, **For the iPhone, then the Mac**, **Done for the iPhone, now for the Mac**, **For the Mac**; a game whose IPA is up to date and whose app for the Mac is to build or failed says so, **Its IPA is up to date, its app for the Mac is to build**, with **Build its app for the Mac**, which asks the Mac for that app alone, as the Build button of its sheet on the Mac does (`due` in the `build` request, [`CartoucheBridge.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBridge.swift)), and **Build what is due** counts it, as the Mac's does, the line under it saying that only a game's app for the Mac can be left to build. **All the Mac's builds** says it of the game building, of each game waiting, of each failure, **Failed for the Mac**, a game's app for the Mac failing apart from its IPA, and of each game of the Mac's list, **Up to date, built yesterday · For the iPhone and the Mac** ([`CartoucheMacBuildsView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheMacBuildsView.swift), [`CartoucheMacBuildsPage.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheMacBuildsPage.swift)). The step of a build of the Mac's app says "for the Mac" on the iPhone, where the Mac's window says "this Mac". The words are the same on both sides, the Mac's "this Mac" being the iPhone's "the Mac", in English and in French, and an iPad says the iPad where they say the iPhone, as its other words do ([`CartoucheDeviceWords.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheDeviceWords.swift)).

A Mac a little older says none of it, and Cartouche shows its builds as it did. An older Cartouche reads a game whose app for the Mac alone is due as up to date, as the Mac now says it to every iPhone, and its **Build again** builds the IPA again with the app, as it did. The galleries of both apps show every case, with data of their own. None of it has been tried on a real Mac or iPhone yet.

This amends [decision 96](096.md).

**Why.** Ad, on 2026-11-17: "From the iOS app's settings or the Mac builder, there is no distinction whether it builds for iOS or Mac." The iPhone said of every build that it was on the Mac, a place, which reads as a build for the Mac; the Mac's window said which part a build made only while it made both, as a word before its step, and nothing of the games waiting nor of the builds done. A game whose IPA was up to date and whose app for the Mac was not showed as up to date on the iPhone, and its button built the IPA again, the Mac's time for nothing, and a failure of the app for the Mac showed nowhere on the iPhone. Ad asked for the whole of the iPhone's app on the Mac, with each of its changes (2026-11-13): the same lines say the same thing on both, each in its own place's words.
