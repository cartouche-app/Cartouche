# 135. nxnative's page also sums up the games played on players' Macs, and the Mac apps built there

2026-11-18

nxnative's weekly page ([`nxnative.py`](../../telemetry/nxnative.py), [telemetry/README.md](../../telemetry/README.md#nxnatives-page)) becomes "nxnative's games on players' iPhones and Macs". After the iPhones' sections, it sums up the games played on players' Macs, in nxnative's app for the Mac, as Cartouche Builder tells how each play ended from the last line of nxnative's launcher in its log (`builder.played`): how many plays, of how many games, on how many Macs, and by game, those three Macs or more played, how their plays ended; what Cartouche's console on the Mac measured of them, by game, with the iPhones' figures but the heat, which nxnative does not read on the Mac; and the Mac apps built on players' Macs, by nxnative commit (`builder.macBuilt`, `builder.macFailed`), apart from the IPAs, as [decision 95](095.md) keeps them. Both kinds of builds also say, every commit together, how many built, of how many games, on how many Macs and in how long, how many failed, on how many Macs, and the steps three Macs or more failed at. A Mac counts as an install does: every line and every figure sums up at least three Macs, a game, a commit or a step fewer share is not named, and the Macs that follow a fork of nxnative are left out, of the plays and of the Macs' sessions too, as are the builds with changes of their own. This amends [decision 82](../decisions.md), whose page summed up the iPhones' sessions alone and set the Macs' apart.

**Why.** Ad, 18 November 2026, as the message for nxnative's Discussions was being written: "oui les parties sur iphone et mac, et les builds". nxnative builds an app for the Mac, and the message gave them that week's figures of it, 58 plays of 10 games on 12 Macs and 37 Mac apps built: the page it points to now has them each Thursday, under the same rules as the iPhones' figures, rather than in a post alone. No Mac has sent a session measured by the console yet: that section says so until three Macs have measured a game.
