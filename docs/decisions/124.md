# 124. Each copy of a game's saves names the version of the game that wrote it, and an older version of the game never takes it

2026-11-17

Each copy of a game's saves in the saves folder names the version of the game that wrote its saves, the game's `CFBundleShortVersionString` as nxnative writes it in the IPA and in the app for the Mac, in the zip's comment, `{"gameVersion":"1.2.0"}`, read from the copy's end with its central directory ([`CartoucheSaveSync.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSaveSync.swift), [`CartoucheZip.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheZip.swift)). A game older than the newest copy's, by its version's numbers, never takes it: not when it is installed or starts, nor over the saves played on it meanwhile, which go to the saves folder as a copy of their own date under the newest one, the newest staying the newer version's; and the copy is not put back on it. The game's sheet says the newest copy comes from a newer version of the game and comes once the game is updated, on the iPhone ([`CartoucheStorageSaves.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheStorageSaves.swift), [`CartoucheSaveBackups.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSaveBackups.swift)) as on the Mac, in the console and in the builder's window ([`MacSaves.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/MacSaves.swift)); putting the copy back says to update the game first, without a report. Once the game is updated, the copy comes as any other. A copy that names no version, as one an older Cartouche or Cartouche Builder made, a game whose version is unknown, or a version that does not read as numbers, holds nothing back, as before. The builder's telemetry tells a copy held back once, as the step `heldBack` of `builder.saves`. The galleries show the sheet of a game whose newest copy waits, on the iPhone and in the Mac's console. It narrows [decision 51](../decisions.md). None of it has been tried on a real iPhone or Mac yet.

**Why.** Ad's iPhone and Mac can hold two versions of a game at once: the builder builds the Mac's app and the IPA from the same files, but one of them can be installed or updated later than the other. A save a newer version wrote may not read on an older one, and nxnative's own save sync knows nothing of versions. The older game keeps playing with its own saves, and loses nothing of either: the newer version's progress stays the newest copy, for the game once updated, and what was played on the older one is kept beside it, for the player to put back. A version in the comment needs no file in the copy, which would change what it holds, and the readers since Cartouche 0.11.2 take an archive with a comment.
