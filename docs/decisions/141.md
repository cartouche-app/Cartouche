# 141. Cartouche's own limit is the highest it measured on the same iPhone, iOS and signature, measured again after it comes to the front

2026-11-24

Cartouche no longer takes its own memory limit, the one a game has in its place, from one reading as the console opens. The console measures it again every 2 s for half a minute after it comes to the front ([`CartoucheOwnMemoryWatch`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheRunningGame.swift)), as do the System page and Play, and Cartouche keeps the highest limit it measured, from one launch to the next, with the iPhone's model, its iOS and whether its signature has Increased Memory Limit; another iOS or signature forgets it ([`CartoucheOwnLimitRecord`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGamePlace.swift)). Cartouche's place has that limit, or the one measured now when higher: on a game's sheet, the memory a game may use and the line of Increased Memory Limit; and where Play starts a game, behind the console or in Cartouche's place, and what follows when iOS stops it there. What the System page shows as the limit for Cartouche, and what a report says, stay the reading of the moment. This amends [decision 48](../decisions.md).

**Why.** Game Mode raises Cartouche's own limit a moment after Cartouche comes to the front: on the iPhones with 12 GB, Cartouche read 6,144 MiB 6 times in 10 as its console opened (998 of 1,653 `app.opened`), and 8,192 nearly every time a moment later, as when the player imports a game (724 of 749), while games in its place had 8,192 at their peak 466 times in 503, 85 of them above 6,144 (telemetry, 24 November 2026). Read once as the console opened, the sheet of a game said it could use 6,144 MiB with Cartouche kept open behind it or not, and the player saw Mario Kart World, up to 8,183 MiB, not fit, while it had 8,192 in Cartouche's place. A game a shortcut starts as Cartouche opens was judged by the same reading: Cartouche's place counted as having no more than behind the console, and the game went there. The highest reading is what a game has in Cartouche's place; one lower, in the background or before Game Mode came, says nothing more.
