# 152. Cartouche speaks Spanish and German everywhere, and its telemetry says the device's own language

2026-11-24

Cartouche speaks Spanish and German besides English and French, everywhere a player reads it: the app on the iPhone and the iPad ([`Cartouche.xcstrings`](../../launcher/Sources/Cartouche.xcstrings)), Cartouche on the Mac, its console ([`strings.json`](../../builder/mac/console/strings.json)) and its own windows (`tr(en, fr, es, de)`, [`L10n.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/L10n.swift)), the builder's engine, whose messages the Mac shows ([`texts.py`](../../builder/cartouche_builder/texts.py)), the Cartouche Installer ([`locales/`](../../installer/src/locales)) and the site, whose header offers the four languages ([`site/`](../../site/README.md)). Each follows the device's language, as English and French did: the first of the iPhone's, the Mac's, the PC's or the browser's languages that it has, else English. The guides stay in English and French, and the site's link to the getting started guide says it is in English. So do the release notes, until a version's come in Spanish or German too, as `<version>.es.md` and `<version>.de.md`, which Cartouche shows as it shows the French ([`CartoucheReleaseNotes.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheReleaseNotes.swift)). The words Cartouche keeps in every language stay as they are: Cartouche, Cartouche Cloud, nxnative, the Builder and the Console of the Mac's switch.

Each report of Cartouche, of its console on the Mac and of the builder now says the device's own language, `deviceLanguage`, beside `language`, the one Cartouche shows: the first of the device's languages, its language and script only (`es`, `de`, `zh-Hans`), never its region, which the server refuses ([`CartoucheGameLanguage.deviceLanguage`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGameLanguage.swift), [`worker.js`](../../telemetry/worker.js)). The dashboard shows the iPhones' beside Cartouche's, and the Macs' under its Macs. This amends [decision 40](../decisions.md): the site is no longer in French and English alone.

Amended by [decision 175](175.md): the site publishes its page once in each language, each at its own address, and its language buttons lead from one to the other.

Not tried on a device yet: the texts in Spanish and German were checked in the tests and in the screenshots of each app, the site in Chromium, not read by a player of either language.

**Why.** Ad asked on 24 November 2026 whether new languages would help, and chose Spanish and German. The telemetry could not say which languages the players speak: its `language` was Cartouche's, English or French. The language each game was started in stood for it, as Cartouche starts a game in the first of the iPhone's languages that the game has: of the 136 iPhones and iPads that played, by their last session, 96 played in English, 15 in French, 9 in Spanish, on 6 models, and 7 in German, on 5, and 9 in 7 other languages, none of them 3 times. A language the games do not have, such as Polish or Turkish, could not show that way: `deviceLanguage` says it, so that the next language is chosen from what the players' devices speak. Its region is left out because the choice of a language does not need it.
