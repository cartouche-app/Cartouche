# 125. A paired iPhone and Mac keep the same connections to the player's storage, passwords included, and the console on the Mac has the iPhone's Storage page

2026-11-17

Once an iPhone is paired with Cartouche Builder, the iPhone and the Mac keep the same connections to the player's storage, over the bridge's encryption: the shared folders over SMB, the S3 buckets, the FTP and SFTP servers and the Google Drives, with their passwords, secret keys, SFTP servers' keys and Google sign-ins ([`CartoucheConnections.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheConnections.swift), the bridge's `connections`, which a Mac's info offers as `hasConnections`). The iPhone sends its connections, with those it removed, once it is paired, two seconds after the player changes one, and as Cartouche comes to the front, every ten minutes at most; the Mac takes them and answers with its own, which the iPhone takes in turn. Of the same connection, the one changed last wins, on either side, a secret it does not give staying the other's; of two connections to the same place, added on both sides before either saw the other, both keep the one whose ID sorts first, and the folders chosen on the other move to it; a removal goes across, and is forgotten after 180 days. What belongs to one device stays there: a paired Mac and a folder picked in the Files app on an iPhone, and the builder's own folders on the Mac.

On the Mac, the console's **System** page opens the iPhone's **Storage** page, with its forms, linked to SMBClient and swift-nio-ssh as the app is ([`ConsoleSystem.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/CartoucheConsole/Mac/ConsoleSystem.swift), [`ConsoleStorage.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/CartoucheConsole/Mac/ConsoleStorage.swift)): the connections, the same as the paired iPhones', and **This Mac** in place of the iPhone's games folder and saves folder, the builder's own, which the player changes in the builder. The Mac keeps the passwords, secret keys and Google sign-ins in `connections.json`, a file of the builder's folder that only the player's account can read, never in its preferences nor in the Mac's keychain, as the owner chose (2026-11-17); the iPhone keeps them in its keychain, as before. The Mac does not use the connections yet for the games and saves it reaches itself: that is the next step. It amends [decision 23](../decisions.md) and [decision 76](../decisions.md). Amended by [decision 131](131.md): the Mac keeps its games' saves in the iPhone's saves folder, and sends its games to the iPhone's games folder, when they are on the player's connections. None of it has been tried on a real iPhone or Mac yet, and the Google Drive sign-in on the Mac, with the player's OAuth client of the iOS type and the builder's bundle identifier, has not been tried at all.

**Why.** Ad asked that adding an iPhone to the Mac shares their network connections, for one library (2026-11-13), and that the whole of the iPhone's app comes to the Mac. A connection typed once is then on each device, and a password changed on one is changed on the others. The change made last wins, as for the saves: the player's last word stands. In the Mac's keychain, macOS would ask the player to allow the builder again after each of its updates, as it is signed ad hoc; a file only the player's account reads keeps them from the Mac's other accounts, as the builder's pairing keys in `bridge.json` are, and never asks. Nothing else sees them: the bridge carries them encrypted between paired devices, and no report or measure ever holds one.
