# 148. The Mac's app is Cartouche, as on the iPhone; what its installed copies update by stays

2026-11-24

The Mac's app is called Cartouche, in `Cartouche.app`, as on the iPhone: the Finder, the Dock, Launchpad, Spotlight, its menu bar, its window, its notifications and its texts say Cartouche ([`AppName.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/AppName.swift), [`bundle.sh`](../../builder/mac/bundle.sh)). Where the site, the guides or a report must tell it from the iPhone's, it is Cartouche for Mac (Cartouche pour Mac); its building part, beside the console, is the Builder. The iPhone speaks of Cartouche on your Mac, and its storage page offers **Add your Mac**.

What the copies already installed update by does not change ([`test_updates.py`](../../builder/tests/test_updates.py)): the releases `builder-v<version>` of this repository, the disk image `Cartouche-Builder-<version>.dmg` and its `.sha256`, the bundle identifier `io.github.clabeuhtegrite.cartouche-builder`. Such a copy updates itself in its place, `Cartouche Builder.app`, as before; at its first start as Cartouche, before anything else, it moves to `Cartouche.app` beside it and opens again from there with the arguments it had, its login item asked again for its new place. It stays where it is on its disk image, in a folder only an administrator may change, or beside a `Cartouche.app` already there, and says Cartouche all the same.

Its folder on the Mac, `~/Library/Application Support/Cartouche Builder`, keeps its name, with its settings, its games' records, its builds and the key of each iPhone paired with it, as do the engine's command, `cartouche-builder`, and the names of its measures (`builder.*`).

**Why.** On the iPhone and on the Mac, a player has one app, which plays their games with the same saves: two names said there were two apps. Ad asked for it on 24 November 2026, as the last step of the Mac's chantier on the roadmap. Changing what the copies already installed look for would leave them without updates, as the repository's move did for Cartouche Builder 0.5.0 and older (decision 117); a copy that renames itself at its first start brings the Finder's name along with no step of the player's.
