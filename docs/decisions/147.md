# 147. The setup's last step is the automation that renews Cartouche every night, which Cartouche sees run

2026-11-24

With the Apple ID's signing, the setup has a fourth step, after LocalDevVPN: **Renew every night**, the automation of Shortcuts that renews Cartouche every night, even while it stays closed. iOS lets no app make it, so the step opens its page, which shows it as Shortcuts shows it once made (every day at 4:00, *Enable LocalDevVPN*, then *Refresh All Apps* of Cartouche), the four steps that make it, **Open Shortcuts**, and **I made the automation**.

Cartouche tells whether the automation renews it from each run of its *Refresh All Apps* action, which now notes in Cartouche's defaults when it started, whether LocalDevVPN's tunnel was up once it had waited for it, and how it ended, with the built-in SideStore's words when it failed ([LiveContainer patch 0018](../../app/patches/livecontainer/0018-note-each-run-of-refresh-all-apps.patch), [`CartoucheShortcutRun.swift`](../../launcher/SideStoreSupport/CartoucheShortcutRun.swift)); and from the player's word, **I made the automation**, until its first night. The step, the row of the System page and the page say where it stands ([`CartoucheNightlyRenewal`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheRenewalRules.swift)):

- **To make**, before any run and any word of the player's.
- **Made**, from the player's word until a run comes, a night and a half at most; then **Not running**: its time, or *Run Immediately*, to check in Shortcuts. Made again after a run, it waits again for its next night.
- **On**, when its last run renewed Cartouche: it ended without an error, or it failed on its way back once the signature was renewed for seven days from the run, as SideStore records it.
- **Failed**, when its last run did not renew Cartouche: without LocalDevVPN connected, whatever SideStore said; ended by iOS first, ten minutes on; or with SideStore's error, in Cartouche's words as for **Renew now**.
- **Not running**, when its last run is 3 days old or more, whatever it said.

The step's button makes, fixes or shows the automation, as it stands. The step is done while the automation is made, running or on; Cartouche still plays without it: the banner over the games, the setup's row in System and `setUp` in the measurements keep to the first three steps, and the setup's top says one last step is left. With the player's own certificate, which Cartouche never renews, there is no such step. *Refresh All Apps* is the automation's alone: **Renew now** and Cartouche's own renewals go through the bridge ([`CartoucheRefresh`](../../launcher/SideStoreSupport/CartoucheRefresh.swift)) and note nothing. `app.opened` sends `nightly`, where the automation stands, and `nightlyProblem`, what its last run failed on (`vpn`, `interrupted`, `sideStore`), never SideStore's words nor when it ran. This amends [decision 88](../decisions.md).

Not tried on an iPhone yet: neither a run of the automation noted, nor the page's steps followed in Shortcuts on iOS 27.

**Why.** Ad asked for it on 24 November 2026, among the next projects: a player new to Cartouche must make the automation by hand, and that is where signatures expire; iOS prevents Cartouche from making it, but Cartouche can guide it. No gesture makes it for the player: iOS lets no app create or open a personal automation, nor open Shortcuts on its Automation tab; a shared shortcut, by an iCloud link or a `.shortcut` file, names *Refresh All Apps* by the bundle identifier and the team of the app, which differ for each player's Cartouche, signed for their own Apple ID, so no one link can hold it, and iOS refuses a shortcut file it did not sign since iOS 15. Nor does Cartouche see the player's automations; but *Refresh All Apps* runs in Cartouche's own process (SideStoreSupport), where it can note itself. Signed for seven days with a free account, a Cartouche without the automation renews itself only when it comes to the front in its last 3 days (decision 88): a player who does not open it for a week loses it, and only the automation keeps it renewed with no one opening it. Up to 0.15.1, the System page explained the automation in five steps and never said whether it had been made, and the measurements could not tell it from Cartouche's other renewals. A night and a half lets an automation made in the afternoon run once before Cartouche says it does not; 3 days keep an iPhone that stayed off two nights from being told its automation stopped. A run that never ended, ten minutes on, was ended by iOS: the longest renewal measured took 71 seconds.
