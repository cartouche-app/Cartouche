# 170. Back to the start of a game: Put back a copy lists the copies nxnative makes on the device as each session starts, and puts one back as it puts back a copy of the saves folder

2026-11-26

Since 26 November (`b9d0f832`), nxnative copies the save each session of a game starts from, before the game writes, into the game's `save-backups/<date> start`, beside its save: `save`, `save_device` when the console's save has files, the size records nxnative keeps beside them (`save.size`, `save_device.size`) and a `.hash` of its own (`dm/host/save_sync.c`, `keep_start_copy`). It makes one only when the save changed since the newest one, keeps up to ten within 256 MB, always the newest, with or without its cloud saves, on the iPhone, the iPad and the Mac (`~/Library/Application Support/nxnative/<title ID>/save-backups`); `DM_SAVE_BACKUPS=0` turns them off. Its own documentation says to go back by copying the folders over the save, the game quit.

**Put back a copy**, on a game's sheet, now lists those copies under **On this iPhone** (**On this Mac** in the console, **On this iPad** on an iPad), each by its date, marked **At the start of a game**, with the size of its saves, below the copies of the saves folder (**In your saves folder**). It lists there too the copies Cartouche keeps of the saves a copy put back replaced, `<date> before putting back`, marked **Before a copy was put back**: putting a copy back can then be undone without a saves folder. The sheet offers **Put back a copy** without a saves folder when the device holds such copies, and on the Mac for a game nxnative's own cloud saves keep ([`CartoucheSwitchSave.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSwitchSave.swift), `copiesHere`, `hasCopiesHere`; [`CartoucheSavesView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSavesView.swift)).

One is put back after a question, the way a copy of the saves folder is, in the turn of the saves' syncs, never while the game runs: the saves here go to the saves folder first, when there is one and they changed since they were last there; they are kept on the device in `<date> before putting back`, there or not; then the copy's `save` and `save_device` folders take the place of the game's, dated at that moment, each with the copy's size record beside it, or without one when the copy has none, the one there kept with its folder, as nxnative puts back a save of its own (its pull, `60f0fe50`); and the copy put back goes to the saves folder as the newest copy, which the player's other devices take ([`CartoucheSaveBackups.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSaveBackups.swift), `putBack(here:)`; on the Mac, [`ConsoleSaves.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/CartoucheConsole/Mac/ConsoleSaves.swift), in the Builder's turn, which syncs first and sends after). nxnative's copy is copied, never moved: it stays as nxnative made it, to come back to again, and nxnative prunes it as it prunes its others. A folder the copy does not hold stays as it is: the console's save when it was empty at that start, and the game's other profiles (`save_user2` to `save_user8`, `save.users`), which nxnative's copies do not hold. Nothing comes while nxnative's pull of its cloud's save is unfinished (`save.pull`). Cartouche's own work goes through a folder of its own in its temporary folder, never in `save-backups`, where nxnative clears only its own staging.

No telemetry is added: putting back a copy of the saves folder is not counted either. No entitlement, no extension and no Info.plist key. None of it has been tried on a real iPhone or Mac yet: [`CartoucheSwitchSaveTests`](../../launcher/Tests/CartoucheCoreTests/CartoucheSwitchSaveTests.swift) checks the listing and the putting back on files laid out as nxnative lays them out, [`ConsoleSavesTests`](../../builder/mac/Tests/CartoucheConsoleTests/ConsoleSavesTests.swift) the Mac's turn, and [`SavesUITests`](../../launcher/Gallery/UITests/SavesUITests.swift) puts one back in the gallery.

**Why.** A save a mod or a session broke cost the player everything since the saves folder's last copy, or all of it without a saves folder, while nxnative already kept the save of each start on the device, out of the player's reach unless they found `save-backups` in Files. Ad chose it on 26 November 2026 among what nxnative offers that Cartouche did not take ("Toutes les six"). The same way as the saves folder's copies, so that the saves put back are never the only ones lost.
