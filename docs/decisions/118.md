# 118. Cartouche sends a Mac whose app no longer updates itself the link to download it again

2026-11-15

A Mac paired with Cartouche whose Cartouche Builder is too old to share its builds, 0.5.0 or older, is one that no longer finds its updates by itself ([decision 117](117.md)). On a game's sheet and on the page of the Mac's builds, Cartouche says so, where it said to update the app, and **Send the link to <Mac>** shares, by AirDrop, Messages or Mail, the link to the disk image of Cartouche Builder 0.6.0, with what to do with it ([`CartoucheMacBuildsView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheMacBuildsView.swift)): the first version that finds its updates where the repository lives since it moved, and which updates itself to the newest once open. The release `builder-v0.6.0` and its disk image stay published for as long as such Macs run ([`test_updates.py`](../../builder/tests/test_updates.py)). On a Mac, the console is the app that builds, newer than 0.5.0 by definition: nothing to mirror there.

**Why.** Ad, 2026-11-15: the Builders already installed must update themselves without going through the site, and as 0.5.0 and older cannot (117), Ad chose that the iPhone give their players the link. The iPhone told them to update an app that said it was up to date. It knows such a Mac by the bridge alone: only 0.6.0 and later say `hasBuilds`, and the bridge says no version. The newest disk image's link changes with each release, and the iPhone would have to find it on GitHub as the Mac's app does; 0.6.0's never changes, and the app takes the newest from there by itself.
