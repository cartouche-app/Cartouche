# 150. The Builder builds for the paired iPhone, or a model chosen, and from nxnative's no-bc variant for one without BC

2026-11-24

nxnative refuses a GPU that reads no BC-compressed texture, the A16's and older (the iPhone 15 and 15 Plus, the iPhone 14 and earlier, the iPad with the A16 and older iPads with an A chip): every game says so on its screen. The `no-bc` branch of [mianmuhammadse/nxnative](https://github.com/mianmuhammadse/nxnative/tree/no-bc) stores those textures otherwise on such a GPU, BC1 to BC3 as ASTC 4x4 and the other formats and 3D textures decompressed, for the games drawn with NVN and, since 22 November 2026, with Vulkan; on a GPU that reads BC it takes nxnative's own path (`DM_TEST_NO_BC || ![device supportsBCTextureCompression]`, in its `nvn_mtl.m`). Cartouche reads which build converts them and lifts their warning ([decisions 137](137.md) and [145](145.md)).

The Builder of Cartouche for Mac now has a setting, **Settings › Builds › Build for** (`build_for` in its settings): the paired iPhones, by default, or a model chosen in a list, iPhone 11 to iPhone Duo and the iPads. Cartouche tells its paired Mac its model identifier, the one its measures carry, and whether its GPU reads BC, with its info requests, encrypted, and nothing else ([`CartoucheBridge.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBridge.swift), `CartoucheBridgeDevice`); the Mac keeps them with the paired iPhone in `bridge.json`, which the engine reads for them alone, never the keys. When the model chosen reads no BC, or, with none chosen, one of the paired iPhones says its GPU reads none, the games are built from the variant, for every iPhone; otherwise from nxnative ([`build_for.py`](../../builder/cartouche_builder/build_for.py), and its copy for the settings' words, [`BuildFor.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/BuildFor.swift), checked against the engine's answers). A repository or branch chosen under **Advanced** wins over both. Each build records the nxnative it was made from (`engine`, its repository and branch); an IPA made from another one than the one in use now is built again (reason `engine`), from the variant once an iPhone without BC is paired, from nxnative again once none is; a build made before builds said it counts as made from the one the settings named. A game built for the Mac alone stays as it is, a Mac's GPU reading BC. The telemetry's `forkOfNxnative` counts the variant as a fork, so the nxnative page leaves those Macs aside as before; `noBC` says the variant, `buildFor` whether the paired iPhones or a model chose it, never which.

On an iPhone without BC, the question before an install and the note in **System** add that Cartouche on the Mac builds its games from the variant once the iPhone is paired with it.

The setup assistant downloaded nxnative from its default repository at the tools step, before any setting could name another: the variant's author, trying his own branch, wrote the settings file by hand before the app's first start. The tools step now says, under nxnative's row, which repository and branch it downloads (`BuildFor.repository`, checked against the engine's answers), and its **Change…** opens Build for and the repository and branch of **Advanced** there; what is chosen before the download is what the step downloads (`api setup` fetches the nxnative the batch would build from).

**Why.** The variant's author asked on Cartouche's Discord for the warning to go for builds of his; Ad asked, on 24 November 2026, for players to make their games that way, and for a setting where the player chooses a model or takes the paired iPhone's. The variant's author, having used the Builder with his branch, asked on 24 November for the repository to be chosen on the tools step. Choosing for the player, from the iPhone it builds for, keeps the variant to the iPhones that need it, nxnative's own for every other, with no repository to type; the variant follows nxnative, and its author merges it regularly, but nxnative's own stays ahead of it.
