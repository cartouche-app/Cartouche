# 130. contact@cartouche.cloud is forwarded by Cloudflare's Email Routing, whose records stay its own

2026-11-17

The owner set up `contact@cartouche.cloud` in Cloudflare's Email Routing, which forwards the mail it receives to the owner's mailbox. Email Routing keeps its records at the apex itself, locked in Cloudflare's dashboard: three MX records, `route1` to `route3.mx.cloudflare.net`, its SPF record, `v=spf1 include:_spf.mx.cloudflare.net ~all`, and its DKIM key, `cf2024-1._domainkey`. They are not in [`domain/records.json`](../../domain/records.json): the Domain workflow leaves them as they are and lists them ([domain/README.md](../../domain/README.md#mail)). The file no longer has its SPF record, `v=spf1 -all`, and the script stops before changing anything where an SPF record of the file's would stand beside another. The file keeps DMARC, `p=reject`. This narrows [decision 128](128.md), where the zone's records are the file.

**Why.** The owner made the address on 2026-11-17. Email Routing's records are what Cloudflare checks before it forwards anything, with MX priorities it picks and a key it makes: kept in the file too, they would be changed from two places, the dashboard and the workflow. A name has one SPF record: beside ours, Email Routing's would make mail servers take neither, and the mail it forwards could be refused. The domain still sends no mail of its own, so DMARC still has mail that claims to come from `cartouche.cloud` refused, unless SPF or DKIM vouches for it.
