# 173. nxnative's newest settings in the player's language, its Polish and Thai, and its Rotation behind the console

2026-11-26

**The settings' words.** Cartouche shows the settings nxnative writes into each game (`dm/launcher.json`, from its `dm/games/settings.tsv`) on its own page of the game's settings, on the iPhone, the iPad and in the console of the Mac, each word in the player's language when it knows it (`CartoucheText.gameSettingText`). Thirteen words of nxnative's list at its commit 2f959ede (27 November 2026) showed in English: **Rotation** and its **Automatic**, **Landscape** and **Portrait**; **Texture filtering**, its **Game default** and its line; **Microphone**, its line, and **Microphone gain**; the newer line of **Picture shape**, and its **MacBook (16:10)** and **16:9 (Switch)**. They now read in English, French, Spanish and German. [`app/scripts/game_setting_texts.py`](../../app/scripts/game_setting_texts.py) lists the words of a `settings.tsv` that Cartouche does not know, and `app/tests/test_game_setting_texts.py` fails on one of the list at 2f959ede; the weekly watch of what nxnative tells the apps that start it (decision 172) says each new word of its newest list. A frame rate, a multiplier (**16x**) and **MetalFX** read the same in every language.

**Polish and Thai.** nxnative reads Polish and Thai from a game's control data since its commit 79ac8c89 (19 November 2026): Super Mario Bros. Wonder 1.2.1 has both. Cartouche starts a game in the first of the iPhone's languages that the game has ([`CartoucheGameLanguage.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGameLanguage.swift)), and Cartouche Builder in the first of the Mac's: an iPhone or a Mac in Polish or Thai started such a game in its own language, as Cartouche did not count them among the Switch's. It now starts it in Polish or Thai. Cartouche Builder's choice of the language a build is made in is left as it was: Cartouche chooses again from the iPhone's languages at each start.

**Rotation behind the console.** nxnative's **Rotation** (`DM_ROTATION`, its games built since 23 November 2026, iPhone and iPad) is **Automatic**, the default, **Landscape** or **Portrait**, on its settings screen, on Cartouche's page of the game's settings and in its in-game menu. In Cartouche's place, where the game is the app, nxnative's own app delegate and view controllers turn it as Rotation says, and Cartouche's code in the game's process, which allows upright too ([decision 103](103.md)), adds upright only for a view controller that allows it itself, which Landscape's do not: Landscape stays sideways, Portrait upright, Automatic upright too, as before. Behind the console, where Cartouche turns the game's screen, Rotation turned nothing: the screen turned upright too, a game locked sideways included. The screen now turns as the game's Rotation says ([`CartoucheGameOrientation.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheGameOrientation.swift), `CartoucheGameRotation`): Automatic as decision 103 says, Landscape sideways only, Portrait upright only (never upside down on an iPhone, upright either way on an iPad), and sideways while the game's picture is on a TV, as before. Play starts the game with the Rotation an env file sets, else the player's choice in its preferences, else the list's default. Then Cartouche's code in the game's process says the Rotation the game has now in its readings, every 2 seconds (`rotation` in `GameMemory.plist`, [`CartoucheGuest.m`](../../launcher/LiveProcessSupport/CartoucheGuest.m)), and the screen follows a choice made in the in-game menu within 3 seconds, at once as the game comes back from the console. A game built before nxnative had Rotation turns as before. The Mac needs nothing: Rotation is the iPhone's and the iPad's, and a Mac's screen does not turn.

The launcher's harness checks the start and the in-game menu's choice behind the console (`UprightTests`); none of it has been tried on a real iPhone yet.

This amends [decision 103](103.md): a game whose Rotation is Landscape or Portrait no longer turns upright too, or sideways, behind the console.

**Why.** Ad, 26 November 2026, of the six things nxnative offers that Cartouche did not take: "Toutes les six". A setting Cartouche shows reads in the player's language and does what it says, wherever the game runs.
