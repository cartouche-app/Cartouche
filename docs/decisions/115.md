# 115. A sign-in that stops for want of the app group, or on a certificate made elsewhere, says what to do in Cartouche's words

2026-11-15

When the built-in SideStore finds no app group to open its database in (`missingAppGroup`), a sign-in, or the registration of the iPhone alone, says what the setup says of Cartouche's IPA for an Apple ID that a signing app signed (`signing.elsewhere`, [decision 113](113.md)), and the sign-in's error offers the same **Download Cartouche `<version>`**, the IPA for the player's own certificate of this version; no longer SideStore's "App Group Container Inaccessible".

When the Apple ID already has a certificate the built-in SideStore does not hold, made by another app or computer, which it never revokes ([patch 0012](../../app/patches/sidestore/0012-find-the-certificate-a-computer-left-in-the-app.patch)), the sign-in says so in Cartouche's words, in the player's language: to sign in with the Apple ID Cartouche was installed with, when it was another; otherwise to install Cartouche again over this copy with the Cartouche Installer and this Apple ID, which gives Cartouche its certificate, then to sign in again (`signIn.otherCertificate`). The setup shows the same words as the reason of a sign-in that stopped before the certificate ([decision 75](../decisions.md)). SideStore's English said nothing of another Apple ID, and ended with the end of each certificate's serial number, which the report no longer shows: the measurements keep the certificates' count, and whether Cartouche carries one from the Installer.

Renewals and updates keep their words.

**Why.** Since 113, a copy signed by a signing app asks for no Apple ID; a sign-in that meets `missingAppGroup` all the same showed SideStore's words, which name an app group and nothing to do. On a certificate made elsewhere, SideStore's words, in English whatever the player's language, sent the player back to the Cartouche Installer and said nothing of the Apple ID: a player who signs in with another Apple ID than the one they installed Cartouche with reads the same as one whose certificate another app or computer replaced, and installing again with the Installer changes nothing for them unless they use the Apple ID they sign in with. Not tried on a real iPhone.
