# 126. Cartouche reaches the player's Mac and servers through Tailscale, which the player installs apart, and renews itself alone only in its last two days while Tailscale is on

2026-11-17

Tailscale is supported, never embedded nor driven: the player installs it from the App Store on the iPhone and on their Mac or NAS, signed in to the same account. Cartouche reads whether its tunnel is up from the device's interfaces, as it reads LocalDevVPN's: an address in 100.64.0.0/10 or fd7a:115c:a1e0::/48 on a tunnel interface, `utun<n>`, a carrier's address in 100.64.0.0/10 on its own interface not counting ([`CartoucheTailscale.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheTailscale.swift)). Nothing is connected to for it, and it needs no permission.

- **The Mac.** Cartouche Builder tells a paired iPhone its addresses in the tailnet in its `info` (`tailscale`); the iPhone keeps them with the Mac (`CartoucheStorageServer.tailnet`), asking again at most once an hour, and, while Tailscale is on here, tries them beside the address it last reached the Mac at, 0.3 seconds after it, the first that opens a session winning and closing the others ([`CartoucheStorageMac.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheStorageMac.swift), `CartoucheBridgeClient.connect(firstOf:)` in [`CartoucheBridge.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBridge.swift)). The Mac's own refusal, as it forgot this iPhone, ends them all. Bonjour does not cross Tailscale: a Mac never met on the Wi-Fi pairs by its Tailscale name (MagicDNS) or address, typed under **Add your Mac › By its address**. Cartouche Builder's **iPhone** page says whether Tailscale is on, with the Mac's address there, or where to get Tailscale for the Mac.
- **The player's other servers.** A NAS, an SFTP, FTP or S3 server is reached by the Tailscale name or address the player types, as any address. The **Storage** page has an **Away from home** section: Tailscale connected or off, and its App Store page.
- **Offline.** A server reached only through Tailscale, by such a name or address, and the Mac whose addresses in the tailnet Cartouche knows, say to turn Tailscale on when they do not answer with it off ([`CartoucheStorageReach.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheStorageReach.swift)); with it on, the Mac says to check Tailscale on both. Tailscale coming on changes the iPhone's network, and Cartouche tries the servers in use again.
- **LocalDevVPN.** iOS keeps one VPN at a time: LocalDevVPN, which Cartouche connects for the built-in SideStore's sign-in, renewals and updates ([decision 98](098.md)), turns Tailscale off, its *On Demand* too, until the player turns it on again, and Tailscale has no link Cartouche could open to do it. So, while Tailscale is on, Cartouche no longer renews itself as it comes to the front in the last 3 days of its signature, only in the last 2, under 48 hours ([`CartoucheRenewalRules.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheRenewalRules.swift)): before, the player's automation renews it at night, and **Renew every night** says to add Tailscale's *Connect* action last, which turns it on again. **Renew now**, a sign-in and an update still connect LocalDevVPN as the player asks for them. Once what needed LocalDevVPN is done, the console's top bar says that Tailscale is off, until it is on again, twelve hours at most, and opens Tailscale's page of the App Store, which opens it (`CartoucheTailscaleReminder`).
- **Reports and measurements.** Reports leave out the tailnet's names (`*.ts.net`, `*.beta.tailscale.net`) and its addresses, and say which tunnel was up (`VPN`: LocalDevVPN, Tailscale or none); `app.opened` and `builder.paired` say whether Tailscale was connected, never the tailnet, its names or its addresses.

On the Mac, the console links the iPhone's storage files, Tailscale's among them, as [decision 95](095.md) asks. None of it has been tried on a real iPhone yet.

This amends [decision 88](../decisions.md): while Tailscale is on, the renewal Cartouche starts by itself waits for the last 2 days of the signature, rather than the last 3, since the LocalDevVPN it connects turns Tailscale off.

**Why.** Ad asked for it on 2026-11-17: "Intégrer Tailscale (installé à part mais pris en charge)", to reach their Mac and their NAS away from home with no step more. Cartouche cannot carry Tailscale's tunnel: an app signed with a free Apple ID gets no Network Extension, and LocalDevVPN, which the built-in SideStore needs, cannot be done without either ([decision 98](098.md)). Reading the interfaces needs no permission and tells LocalDevVPN's tunnel from Tailscale's by their addresses. Asked when Cartouche may turn Tailscale off to renew itself, Ad chose the last two days only (2026-11-17): the player keeps Tailscale through their day, and the night's automation, which renews Cartouche first, turns it on again.
