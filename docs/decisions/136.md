# 136. Cartouche links to a Cartouche Cloud account, follows its games and installs those built

2026-11-18

On the iPhone, **System › Cartouche Cloud** ([`CartoucheCloudPage.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheCloudPage.swift)) says what Cartouche Cloud is, with the price of a build as the service gives it, never written in the app, and links Cartouche to the player's account with the code of the account's page on the site, eight letters and digits valid ten minutes, once: typed on that page, or given by the site's **Open in Cartouche** button or its QR code, `cartouche://cloud/link?code=…`, which opens the page and links at once ([patch 0017](../../app/patches/livecontainer/0017-open-cartouche-clouds-page-with-the-code-of-an-accounts-page.patch)). The service then gives the iPhone a token of its own, 256 bits, which Cartouche keeps in the keychain for this iPhone alone, readable after its first unlock, and shows to `build.cartouche.cloud` alone, with no cookie, no cache and no redirection followed ([`CartoucheCloud.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheCloud.swift), [`CartoucheCloudLink.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheCloudLink.swift)). It reads the account's games and credits and the address of a game built, nothing more: sending a game, the credits and the account stay on the site, where the player signs in by email.

Linked, the page shows the credits, those held for the games under way, and the account's games where each is, in the site's words: being sent, in the queue with its place, being built with its step of six and the time left, built with the moment it is deleted, or not built, with why and what is left to do on the site. It reads them every 10 seconds while a game is on its way, every minute otherwise, and pulls to refresh. A game built shows in the library and on its sheet, as one of the games folder does, with **Install**, which reads its IPA where it sits, from the service's storage, as from the player's NAS, with the same progress, Live Activity and resumption: at an address the service gives for a quarter of an hour, and asks again to go on with a longer download, which the service counts as one. Installed, the game has **Copy to** the games folder, as any game installed from a file. Coming to the front, Cartouche reads the account's games once a minute at most, for a game built meanwhile to show; what it last read stays in its caches, without the token, so that the library shows it as Cartouche opens.

**Unlink this iPhone**, on the page, or Unlink on the account's page, ends the link, and Cartouche forgets the token and what it read, at the platform's first answer that it no longer knows it; a code typed while linked links the iPhone to that account in place of the other. **Reset Cartouche** unlinks the iPhone first, five seconds at most, then erases the token with the storage's items. The token, the account and its games are never in a report, the journal or a measurement: `game.installed` says only where a game came from, `from`, here `cloud` ([telemetry/README.md](../../telemetry/README.md#reports)). The iPhone alone: Cartouche's console on the Mac has no Cartouche Cloud, and a Mac builds its own games with Cartouche Builder.

This amends [decision 1](../decisions.md): Cartouche also downloads the IPA that Cartouche Cloud built for the account the player linked it to, from the copy the player sent there themselves, and nothing else ([LEGAL.md](../../LEGAL.md), [privacy.md](../privacy.md)).

**Why.** Ad, 17 November 2026: the whole of Cartouche Cloud first, then into the app "quand tout sera prêt"; then, on 18 November, Cartouche Cloud on the iPhone alone, and the plan of its part in the app, approved as written. Cartouche Cloud builds the game of a player who has no Mac: without the app, that player would need a computer again to fetch the game built and bring it to the iPhone. A code from the account's page keeps the sign-in on the site, by email, where the account and its credits are, and asks Cartouche for no address nor password; a token that only reads lets a lost iPhone spend nothing, send nothing and change nothing of the account, and the site lists it to unlink. The price comes from the service, so that a new price needs no new version of Cartouche.
