# 153. The games Cartouche installs stay out of the iPhone's backups; their saves stay in them

2026-11-24

The games Cartouche installs are left out of the iPhone's backups, in iCloud or on a computer, always, with no setting ([`CartoucheBackup.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBackup.swift)). Cartouche marks its folder of games, `Documents/Applications`, with all it holds, and each game's app as it installs it or another build of it (`isExcludedFromBackup`); and again each time it starts, for the games installed before it marked them and a folder made again since. In the app group's folder of games, which another LiveContainer of the player's may share, only the games Cartouche lists are marked, never the folder itself. A game's saves, in its data folder, stay in the backups, with all else of Cartouche's, and in the player's saves folder when they chose one. After a restore, a game installs again from the player's storage, as on a new iPhone.

On the Mac, as the rule that the Mac mirrors the iPhone asks ([decision 95](095.md)), the builder leaves out of Time Machine this Mac's copy of a game's app that the games folder keeps in one file, on a NAS: as it installs it to be played, and as it makes it again of each new build, with `tmutil addexclusion`, whose mark goes with the app and needs no administrator ([`macapps.py`](../../builder/cartouche_builder/macapps.py)). The file in the games folder, and an app the games folder keeps as it is, are the player's own and stay in the backups, as do the game's saves in `~/Library/Application Support/nxnative`. A copy installed before is marked when it is installed again or made again of the next build.

**Why.** A player's next iCloud backup was to take 43.74 GB more, the games Cartouche had installed, and iOS showed no switch for Cartouche under iCloud Backup that would let them leave it out: the only ways left were to turn iCloud Backup off for the whole iPhone or to uninstall their games before each backup ([#302](https://github.com/cartouche-app/Cartouche/issues/302)). Nothing in Cartouche, LiveContainer or SideStore marked them. A game installs again from the player's storage, where a backup would only fill their iCloud with a copy of it; their saves cannot be made again. Ad chose on 24 November 2026 to leave the games out always, rather than with a setting that would put them back: one setting fewer. iOS leaves out a folder marked so with all it holds, but the mark belongs to the item it was set on, so a folder removed and made again comes without one, and an app moved elsewhere keeps only its own: hence the folder, each app, and again at each start.
