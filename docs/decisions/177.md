# 177. The home filters the player's games: all of them, those installed, or those not installed

2026-11-27

While the player has games installed and games not installed, whose IPA waits on their Mac, in a games folder or in Cartouche Cloud, a menu beside **My library**, the title of the home's shelf, shows **All** of them, those **Installed** or those **Not installed** ([`CartoucheHomeFilter.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheHomeFilter.swift)). The controller's shoulder buttons go from one to the next on the home, as they change the filter of All games. The filter in use, but All, is drawn in Cartouche's colours, so that a shelf that leaves games out says so. Cartouche keeps the player's choice in its preferences; a player whose games are all installed, or none, sees no menu and every game, whatever they chose before. The covers that import a game and open every game stay at the end of the shelf.

The shelf takes no game away from under the player: a game installed from **Not installed**, or uninstalled from **Installed**, stays there until the player chooses a filter again or the home opens again, so that Play is there for the game just installed. The game paused behind the console shows whatever the filter, with Resume. The focus stays on its game as the shelf changes, or goes to the game that took its place; the player's first game, imported from **Import a game**, comes in focus as before.

The Mac's console has the same home ([decision 95](095.md)): the games built for this Mac are those installed, and those its games folder keeps, not on this Mac yet, those not installed.

The game measurements say each choice, `home.filtered`, with the filter and whether the shoulder buttons chose it ([telemetry/README.md](../../telemetry/README.md#reports)), never a game.

This amends decision 34: the home shows the player's games, installed or in their games folder, as the filter leaves them.

**Why.** A player asked for it on Discord (27 November 2026): their Mac keeps games they do not install, and the shelf mixed those they play with those they could install, Install from their MacBook offered on the game in focus. A player with everything installed has nothing to sort, and a filter left on Not installed would empty their shelf. A game that left the shelf as it was installed would take the focus, and Play, away from the player who just installed it to play it.
