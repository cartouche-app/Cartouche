# 122. On the Mac, a game's sheet in the console shows its saves, their copies and its saves from the Switch, as on the iPhone

2026-11-17

A game's sheet in Cartouche's console on the Mac shows its saves with the iPhone's own screens, which the console now links ([`CartoucheSavesView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSavesView.swift), [`CartoucheSwitchSaveView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSwitchSaveView.swift)): when they were last in step with the saves folder of the builder's **iPhone** page, what the last sync brought and what it kept of this Mac's as a copy, **Sync with … now**, **Put back a copy**, the game's copies, the newest first, one of which is put back after a question, and **Switch saves**, the game's saves between the player's Switch and this Mac, a JKSV backup's ZIP brought in, the saves it replaced put back, and this Mac's saves made into a ZIP for JKSV, which a save panel puts where the player chooses, a file of the same name going to the Trash first. The builder keeps them in step as it did ([decision 95](095.md), [decision 114](114.md)), and the console shows what it did and asks it for the rest ([`ConsoleSaves.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/CartoucheConsole/Mac/ConsoleSaves.swift)): a sync now, the copies, a copy put back, a save from the Switch put in place, each in the builder's turn for the game, after the syncs under way and never under the game running, which is looked at again just before the saves change; a copy put back first sends this Mac's saves when the saves folder does not have them, and goes there as the newest copy, which the iPhone takes at its next start, as Cartouche does on the iPhone. A saves folder that is not there, a NAS's share not mounted, is said as the iPhone says a storage place it cannot reach, in the Mac's words: mount the share in the Finder, and the saves go as soon as it is there. A game whose saves nxnative's own cloud saves keep says so instead. A sync that failed says why, with **Copy the report**. A page the sheet opens has the way back to the game above it, Escape going back too. The console's words are the iPhone's, with the Mac's where the iPhone speaks of itself. The Builder's gallery shows the sheet with its saves, the copies and the Switch's page, with copies of its own, nothing of the player's read or kept. This is the first part of the seventh step of [decision 95](095.md); the console's storage page comes next. None of it has been tried on a real Mac yet.

**Why.** Ad asked for the whole of the iPhone's app on the Mac, with each of its changes (2026-11-13). The builder kept the saves of the games played on the Mac in step with the iPhone's, but only its own window said what they did, in one line: on the console's sheet, the player had no copy to put back and no way to bring a save from their Switch. The iPhone's screens on the Mac keep both alike, and each later change of them comes to the Mac with it. The builder alone touches the Mac's saves, one game's syncs one after the other, so the console asks it rather than changing them itself: a copy put back or a save from the Switch never meets a sync, nor a game started from the Dock meanwhile.
