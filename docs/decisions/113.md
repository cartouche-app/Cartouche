# 113. The IPA for an Apple ID, signed by a signing app, points to the IPA for the player's own certificate

2026-11-15

When Cartouche starts as the IPA for an Apple ID with no app group that LiveContainer finds, it asks for no Apple ID. Only the built-in SideStore and the Cartouche Installer give it that app group; a signing app on the iPhone leaves it out, and the built-in SideStore's sign-in then stops at once (`missingAppGroup`). The setup and the first game's guide put one step in place of LocalDevVPN's and the Apple ID's: it says why signing in cannot work there, and offers **Download Cartouche `<version>`**, the IPA for the player's own certificate ([89](089.md)) of the same version, opened in Safari from its release on GitHub, to sign in the signing app instead, or the Cartouche Installer on a computer ([imported-signing.md](../imported-signing.md), `CartoucheImportedSigning.isSignedElsewhere`). Its words are `signing.elsewhere`, which the sign-in's error for `missingAppGroup` can take as they are. Its screens are in the launcher's gallery (`work: signed-elsewhere`) and its UI tests; no signing app has signed either IPA on an iPhone yet.

**Why.** A signing app signs whatever IPA the player gives it, and the IPA for an Apple ID is the one listed first. Signed that way, Cartouche asked for an Apple ID whose sign-in could only fail, and nothing told the player that another IPA was the one to sign. From the version of November 15, that IPA is published beside each release (89): Cartouche can name it, and take the player to it.
