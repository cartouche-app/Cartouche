# 107. What to do when the built-in SideStore closes, and a launch's wait that always ends

2026-11-14

When the built-in SideStore closes before it finished a sign-in, or the registration of the iPhone alone, Cartouche tells the player what to do: restart the iPhone, then try again, and if it closes again, install the update Settings offers, if there is one; no longer LiveContainer's English sentence. A SideStore that quits as it launches ends the action with that error, at the stage `launch`, where the action waited forever ([patch 0016 of LiveContainer](../../app/patches/livecontainer/0016-end-the-wait-for-a-sidestore-that-quits-as-it-launches.patch)). Renewals and updates keep their words.

**Why.** Issue #179: a sign-in stopped each time as the built-in SideStore quit, and the player read "Built-in SideStore quit unexpectedly", in English whatever their language, with nothing to do; they had tried every way to install Cartouche. Nothing on the iPhone tells Cartouche why SideStore's process ends, which only the iPhone's crash report says: a restart, and an update of iOS where one is offered, are what the player can do, and the issue's reporter is asked for that report. LiveContainer's bridge dropped its wait for SideStore's launch when the process quit before SideStore said it had launched: the sign-in then showed itself under way until the player closed Cartouche, with no error, no report to copy and nothing in the measurements ([decision 35](../decisions.md)). The stage `launch` tells a SideStore that quits as it launches from one that quits on its way. Not tried on a real iPhone.
