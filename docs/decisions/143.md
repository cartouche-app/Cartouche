# 143. The iPhone reaches the player's Mac through its cable too, no Wi-Fi needed

2026-11-24

An iPhone plugged into the player's Mac by a cable reaches Cartouche Builder's bridge through it, as it does on the Wi-Fi: Add your Mac lists that Mac first, **By cable**, pairing with the same six digits on both screens goes through the cable, then the Games and Saves folders, encrypted with the key both keep, as on the network ([`CartoucheBridgeCable.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBridgeCable.swift), one file on both sides). Plugged in, Cartouche signs in through the cable first, then, unplugged, at the Mac's last address, in the tailnet and by Bonjour, as before ([`CartoucheStorageMac.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheStorageMac.swift)); a Mac neither reaches is offline, and says to plug the iPhone in or put it on the Mac's Wi-Fi.

Only the Mac can open a connection through the cable: macOS's usbmuxd, which the Finder uses, opens one to a port of the iPhone plugged in. So Cartouche listens on the iPhone's loopback, port 47921, while it is open ([`CartoucheCableListener.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheCableListener.swift)), and Cartouche Builder, with **Share with Cartouche on the iPhone** on, tries each iPhone plugged in by USB there every two seconds ([`Usbmux.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/Usbmux.swift), [`BridgeCable.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/BridgeCable.swift)). Once Cartouche answers, the Mac keeps that connection, its link: it says which Mac it is, then beats every four seconds, and Cartouche lets a link silent for thirteen seconds go. For each session, Cartouche gives a ticket on the link and the Mac opens another connection that says it; the bridge's own handshake follows on that one. An iPhone the Mac reaches by the network alone (Wi-Fi sync) is left to Bonjour. Cartouche Builder's iPhone page says an iPhone connected by cable. This extends [decision 44](../decisions.md). Extended by [decision 162](162.md): plugged in, a game of the NAS comes through the Mac when the Mac is on its Ethernet.

**Why.** Ad asked for transfers from Cartouche Builder to the app by cable rather than Wi-Fi only (2026-11-24): a Mac and an iPhone on different networks, a guest Wi-Fi or a network that keeps devices apart, left the player with nothing but Tailscale. usbmuxd is part of macOS, so the Mac installs nothing and the player opens no Terminal; listening on its loopback needs no permission of iOS, no entitlement and no paid account. The link and its tickets keep the bridge as it is on both sides: the same pairing, key and folders, whichever way a session comes.
