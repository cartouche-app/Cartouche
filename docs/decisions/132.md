# 132. The game measurements make a game's first sheet, with only what is known of what it needs

2026-11-17

Each Thursday, the Game measurements workflow ([telemetry/README.md](../../telemetry/README.md#game-sheets)) no longer fills only the sheets [`catalog/games/`](../../catalog/games/) has: a game of nxnative's list ([`catalog/nxnative.json`](../../catalog/nxnative.json)) that has none gets one with its first result, once three iPhones or more of one model played it in the 28 days before, a copy of another region counting for its game ([`sheets.py`](../../telemetry/sheets.py)). The sheet is named by the title ID the list gives the game, with its name and platform there, and needs only what is known:

- iOS 26, the oldest system nxnative builds for (`deploymentTarget` in its `dm/ios/project.yml`, for Metal 4, at the commit Cartouche pins);
- BC texture compression, which nxnative needs of every GPU ([decisions.md](../decisions.md), 67);
- Increased Memory Limit when its peak, with the room Cartouche keeps under a limit (an eighth of it, 512 MiB at least: [decisions.md](../decisions.md), 48), does not fit in the most memory iOS gave an app without the capability on a real device ([`catalog/devices.json`](../../catalog/devices.json): 3,376 MiB on an iPhone of 12 GB).

It has no peak of its own beside its results', and no recommended settings: those stay for testers to write. A model none of whose sessions drew a frame, though they lasted 30 minutes or more in all, gives no result, rather than one that says the game does not run: nxnative may write no summary line for the game, from which Cartouche counts frames, as for Super Mario Galaxy, which nxnative's page says since November 17. Any sheet, a tester's too, comes to need Increased Memory Limit once its peak no longer fits without it, and never the other way. A game the list does not have gets no sheet.

The job that pushes the branch `catalog-measurements` takes only the sheets main has, and new ones of games of the list, each named by its title ID (`sheets.py --take`). Its summary lists the new sheets, for a game that gets one changes in Cartouche: its page, on the iPhone and in the Mac's console, gains its verdict for the player's device, what it needs and its measured results; a result that says it plays lists it with the iPhone's games; and on the iPhone, it starts behind the console or in Cartouche's place by its peak (decision 48). So the pull request that adds a sheet shows its screenshots (Launcher screens) to Ad before it is merged, as every change Cartouche shows does.

**Why.** Ad asked why `catalog/games/` had a single game (2026-11-17), and chose to have the Thursday workflow make the others, in time for the version of November 19. By then, the measurements had eleven more games played on three iPhones or more of one model, whose pages said nothing of them, and whose peaks Cartouche did not know as they started: a game whose peak does not fit behind the console went there, and only a stop for lack of memory sent it to Cartouche's place the next time. What a sheet says a game needs, Cartouche checks against the player's device; nxnative's own build settings and the measurements give it, and nothing on a sheet is filled in from a guess ([catalog/README.md](../../catalog/README.md)).
