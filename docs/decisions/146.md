# 146. The games folders and the saves folder chosen on the Mac's console too, the one chosen last winning on every side

2026-11-24

The Storage page of Cartouche's console on the Mac changes the iPhones' games folders and saves folder as the iPhone's page does, on one of the player's connections ([`ConsoleStorage.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/CartoucheConsole/Mac/ConsoleStorage.swift)): **Change folder** for the games folder and for the saves folder, **Add another games folder**, **Stop reading this folder** under another games folder, and **Choose a folder** when none of the iPhones' is on a connection. Cartouche Builder keeps the change as the folders chosen last, chosen at that moment (`CartoucheChosenFolders.changing`, [`CartoucheConnections.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheConnections.swift)), keeps the saves of the Mac's games and sends its builds there at once, and gives the folders it keeps back in its answer to each iPhone's connections (`places`, [`CartoucheBridge.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheBridge.swift)).

An iPhone takes from that answer the games folders, and the saves folder, chosen later than its own, as if chosen there at that moment, so that neither side takes turns ([`CartoucheStorageSettings.take(folders:)`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheStorage.swift)): on its servers, or on the one that replaced a server, and only then. A folder on none of them, or on none at all, as the Mac's own or a folder of Files another iPhone chose, leaves its own as they are; its own games folders on none of the connections, the Mac through the bridge or a folder of Files, stay among its games folders, after those it took. The games folders taken are read again at once. As with the connections, the iPhone takes them when Cartouche comes to the front (at most every ten minutes), after a pairing and after a change made there. A Cartouche Builder older than this one answers with no folders and an older Cartouche reads none: each side then works as before.

The saves folder is changed on the Mac, never stopped there: the Builder's own setting keeps the Mac's saves in its own folder. Nothing of it has been tried on a real Mac or iPhone yet.

It amends [decision 131](131.md), where the folders went from the iPhone to the Mac only.

**Why.** Ad, 24 November 2026: the connections went both ways, but the games folder and the saves folder only from the iPhone to the Mac, whose console said to choose another on the iPhone; Ad asked for them to go both ways too. The rule is the connections' and the saves' own: the newest wins. A folder the Mac cannot see is never taken from an iPhone, which would lose a games folder it reads, nor given to one that cannot open it.
