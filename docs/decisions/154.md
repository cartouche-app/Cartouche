# 154. The apps installed that are not nxnative games are on the System page

2026-11-24

The apps installed in Cartouche that are not nxnative games, an IPA the player imported to try it, are listed near the end of the System page, under **Other installed apps**, after Game measurements and before Cartouche's version, Report a problem and Reset Cartouche; the section shows only when there is one ([`CartoucheSystemView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSystemView.swift), `CartoucheCatalog.otherApps(among:)`). Each has its icon, its name, **Open** and **Uninstall**. **Open** closes the page and starts the app in Cartouche's place, as LiveContainer's Apps tab started it, never behind the console: the console shows games only and would have no card of it to resume or quit it. **Uninstall** asks first, then closes the page and removes the app with its data. Once Cartouche has installed such an app, it says so: "you will find it in System, under Other installed apps". The console stays a console of games: no other app is on it, in All games or elsewhere.

On the Mac, as the rule that the Mac mirrors the iPhone asks ([decision 95](095.md)), nothing changes: its console lists only the games the Builder made for that Mac, and installs no IPA, so it has no other app to show.

This amends [decision 24](../decisions.md), which hid LiveContainer's pages, its Apps tab among them.

**Why.** Ad installed an IPA that is not a game, to try something, and Cartouche said it was "in Apps": there was no Apps anywhere, only All games, Import and System. That message came from LiveContainer's Apps tab, which Cartouche has hidden since 6 November (24), and nothing else listed such an app: it took room on the iPhone, and could be neither opened nor removed but by resetting Cartouche, which removes the games too. Ad chose on 24 November 2026 to list them under "Other installed apps" near the end of the System page, rather than refusing such an IPA or only correcting the message.
