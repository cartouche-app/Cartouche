# 158. The telemetry is in the account of the zone cartouche.cloud

2026-11-24

The telemetry server, its database and its dashboard move from Ad's own Cloudflare account, where they were made on 10 November, to the account that has the zone `cartouche.cloud`, where the project's other Workers already are: Cartouche Cloud, the source for the player's SideStore ([decision 155](155.md)).

- The server answers at **`https://telemetry.cartouche.cloud`**, a name of the project's domain, which no later move of account changes again. The [Telemetry](../../.github/workflows/telemetry.yml) workflow deploys it there with the Domain workflow's token *Cartouche domain*, to which the owner added **Account › D1 › Edit**; it attaches the name itself, and Cloudflare keeps its DNS record, outside [`domain/records.json`](../../domain/records.json). Its database is a new D1 database of that account, in the EU jurisdiction, as before. `CARTOUCHE_TELEMETRY_URL` in [`app/config.env`](../../app/config.env) gives that address to the iPhone and the Mac from the version that follows the move.
- Every Cartouche given out before sends to `https://cartouche-telemetry.perso-3ac.workers.dev`, written in its Info.plist: that address stays valid. In the former account, the Worker of that name becomes a relay ([`telemetry/relay/`](../../telemetry/relay/worker.js)) that passes each request on to the new address and gives its answer back, with no database. It stays there, on that account's free plan, for as long as such copies send to it.
- The [Telemetry move](../../.github/workflows/telemetry-move.yml) workflow ([`telemetry/move.sh`](../../telemetry/move.sh), [telemetry/README.md](../../telemetry/README.md#the-move)) holds the reports at the former address while the database moves, with 503 and a Retry-After the apps wait out with their reports kept, copies every row of the former database into the new one, again until both hold the same rows, table by table, then opens the relay. Once the move is checked, it deletes the former database and dashboard.
- The dashboard is at the `workers.dev` address of the new account, behind Cloudflare Access, which the owner turned on for that account (Zero Trust, Free plan), with the same policy as before.

It amends [decision 35](../decisions.md), where the server was a Worker of the project's own on Ad's account, and [decision 43](../decisions.md), where the dashboard was in the same account as that server: it still is.

**Why.** Ad asked on 24 November 2026 to bring the telemetry and its dashboard back into the account of `cartouche.cloud`: half the project's Workers were on his own account, and that account's free plan, while the account of `cartouche.cloud` is on Workers Paid. A Worker that bears the project's name belongs in the account of its domain. The iPhones already given out cannot learn a new address until they update, so their address answers through the relay, and the reports they keep in their queue while the database moves arrive afterwards: nothing is lost, and nothing is counted twice, each report carrying its own random number.
