# 164. A game's other profiles go and come with its saves, and the shaders it prepared beside them

2026-11-26

A game's copies in the saves folder hold the saves of its other profiles too, 2 to 8, and their registry: `save_user2/` to `save_user8/` and `save.users`, beside `save/` and `save_device/`, as nxnative keeps them (its [docs/frontends.md](https://github.com/seedds/nxnative/blob/main/docs/frontends.md), "Local users and the account selector": to be moved and backed up together, without the working folders `.work` and `.new`). A game no profile was added to has neither, and its copies are as before, byte for byte: nxnative writes `save.users` only when the player adds a profile. A copy that holds no other profile, as one an older Cartouche made, replaces `save/` and `save_device/` only, and leaves the profiles here as they are; one that holds some replaces them all, as the newest save wins. The profiles' saves count in what tells that the saves changed: a second profile that plays makes a copy, while it plays and after, as the first does. Profiles are only ever added, never removed (nxnative's `dm_account_create_user`). The import of a save from the player's Switch and the ZIP for JKSV take the first profile's save alone, as before; the documentation says so.

The list of the shaders and pipelines nxnative built on the spot for the game, `pso_user.tsv` beside `save/`, which it prepares again before the next start, is no save: it stays out of the copies and of what tells that the saves changed, so that it makes no copy of each game and never makes a device's saves look newer than they are. It goes beside the copies, as one file, `<game> [<title ID>]/pso_user.tsv`, which every device adds the lines it lacks to, after its own, and never takes a line from: a list made on one device works on any other (nxnative's docs/development.md, "Prewarm lists"), and lines a build cannot use are left aside. Its lines the device lacks come when the game is installed and before it starts, while it does not run, as after a reinstall or a Reset; what the game prepared since goes after it, never from the copy of the saves made while it plays. A list is read only when its size differs from the device's, and two lists of one size, one holding all of the other's lines, hold the same. A list that cannot move holds nothing up. The pipeline archive beside it, `pso_archive/`, compiled for one GPU, stays on its device.

The same code does it on the iPhone, the iPad and the Mac's console, through the iPhone's saves folder on a connection or a folder of the Mac's ([`CartoucheSaveSync.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheSaveSync.swift), [`CartoucheStorageSaves.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheStorageSaves.swift), [`MacSaves.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/MacSaves.swift)). It amends [decision 51](../decisions.md) and [decision 144](144.md). Nothing new is measured.

An older Cartouche, or an older Cartouche for Mac, leaves `pso_user.tsv` where it is, as it looks at the copies alone; but it refuses a copy that holds other profiles, as holding more than saves, until it is updated: a game with other profiles played on two devices wants both updated. Not tried on a real iPhone or Mac yet.

**Why.** Chosen on 26 November 2026 among the improvements proposed that day. Until now only `save/` and `save_device/` went: a second profile's game was lost at a reinstall or a Reset, and did not follow on the iPad or the Mac. nxnative's own advice for a reinstall is to copy `save/` and `pso_user.tsv` over (docs/playing.md), and the telemetry of the 30 days before shows why: of 90 pairs of a copy and a game with three games at least, the first game was held up 267 ms a minute by shaders (median), the third and later 78 ms, with 31 shader stutters against 12. The list goes beside the copies, merged, rather than in them, so that an older Cartouche still reads every copy, the list of each device adds to the others' rather than the newest replacing them, and it can come even when the saves did not change.
