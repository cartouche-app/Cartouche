# 178. Once the first game is over, the home offers a saves folder while the saves are on this device alone

2026-11-27

Back on the console after the player's first game, once a minute of play is counted on this device, every game's and every data folder's together ([`CartouchePlayTime.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartouchePlayTime.swift), `CartouchePlayTimeRecord.seconds`), the banner at the top of the home says **Keep your saves safe**: "Choose a folder on iCloud Drive, your Mac or a NAS, so that a reinstall loses nothing." A tap opens the Storage page over the console, where the player chooses the saves folder as from System › Storage, in Files (iCloud Drive), on their Mac, their NAS or any other place of theirs: no new way to keep the saves. Its ×, as the banner of Renew every night has ([decision 159](159.md)), takes it off the home for good. It never shows while a game plays behind the console, nor before a minute of play, a game that stopped as it started being no first game ([`CartoucheOffers.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheOffers.swift), `CartoucheSavesOfferRule`).

On the iPhone, it shows while no saves folder is chosen and none ever was: a folder chosen, then stopped, was the player's choice, and the offer stays away. It goes as soon as a saves folder is chosen. The guide's card, and the setup's banner while one of its first three steps or Increased Memory Limit is left, come first; the offer comes before the banner of Renew every night, which shows again once the offer goes ([`CartoucheHomeView.swift`](../../launcher/Sources/CartoucheHomeView.swift), `CartoucheSavesOffer`).

On the Mac, as the rule that the Mac mirrors the iPhone asks ([decision 95](095.md)), the console's home offers it while the Builder keeps the saves in the folder it took by itself, in its own on this Mac's disk (`BuilderHome.saves`), the player having chosen none: there, too, they are on this Mac alone, and a lost disk or a reinstall loses them. Its words say so: "They are on this Mac alone: choose a folder on iCloud Drive or a NAS to keep a copy elsewhere." A tap opens the console's Storage page. A folder the player chose, on the Mac, a NAS or an iPhone's connection, and the Builder's saves turned off, which is the player's choice too, never show it.

The game measurements hear, on the iPhone and on the Mac, that the banner showed (`offer.shown`, once), each tap on it and on its × (`offer.tapped`, `savesBanner` and `savesHidden` at `home`), and, once it showed, the kind of place the player then chose for the saves (`offer.taken`, `kind`: `smb`, `folder`, `s3`, `ftp`, `sftp`, `mac` or `drive`), never its name, its path or its address ([`telemetry/README.md`](../../telemetry/README.md)).

Not tried on an iPhone or a Mac yet: the banner after a real first game.

**Why.** The telemetry of 26 November 2026: 57 of the 135 copies of Cartouche that had a game had no saves folder, so that an uninstall, Reset Cartouche or a lost iPhone erases their progress. Nothing offered one but System › Storage, which a player who plays never opens: the guide's step of a folder is the games folder's. Once a first game is over, the player has saves worth keeping, and is back on the home, where the banner of Renew every night already reaches them. Chosen for the release of 27 November 2026, from the roadmap's ideas kept on 26 November.
