# 114. On the Mac, a game started from Cartouche fills the screen, and the Mac's saves meet the iPhone's by themselves

2026-11-15

A game started from Cartouche Builder, from the console or from a game's page, fills the screen as it starts, as a console's game does, Cartouche's window behind it, and that window comes back in front when the game ends, on the console or the page the game was started from; a window the player closed, a game started from the menu bar, stays closed. The start sets nxnative's Display mode (`DM_FULLSCREEN=1`) unless one is saved for the game, which then holds (nxnative's settings window saves Fullscreen alone, a window being its default), and a start with that window first leaves the choice to it ([`MacPlay.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/MacPlay.swift)); ⌃⌘F still puts the game in a window. Until the player chooses one, the Mac's saves folder is Cartouche's saves folder found on a share mounted on the Mac, the iPhone's on a NAS, as **Use it** on the **iPhone** page makes it: looked for when the builder starts and each time a share is mounted, and taken when it is the only one, and no iPhone paired with the Mac keeps saves of other games in the builder's own folder through the bridge. Saves of a game the Mac kept in step with another folder, and did not change since, are no news for the folder the player chose since: they stay in the other one, and the newest copy of the new one comes to the Mac in their place, as to a Mac without saves; were they no longer in the other folder, they are kept in the new one first, as a copy of their date under its newest ([`MacSaves.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/MacSaves.swift)). Saves the Mac changed since are news there, and the saves written last win, as [decision 51](../decisions.md) has it. A game's switches on its page, built for the iPhone, for this Mac, followed, show the player's choice at once, the library being read again after the engine's answer, as before ([`GameSwitches.swift`](../../builder/mac/Sources/BuilderKit/GameSwitches.swift)). This amends [decision 95](095.md).

**Why.** Ad's first games on a Mac (2026-11-15). The game opened in a window of its own beside Cartouche's rather than in Cartouche: of the game filling the screen with Cartouche behind it, a window beside Cartouche, or the game inside Cartouche's window, Ad chose the first. The game is nxnative's app, a process of its own, which only nxnative could make draw in another app's window. The game started without the iPhone's saves, and Ad found nowhere to put them: the Mac kept its saves in the builder's own folder until the player chose the iPhone's on the **iPhone** page, which the console does not show, and the game had saved anew there. Chosen then, the iPhone's folder would have taken those new saves as its newest copy, written last, for the iPhone to take in place of its own: a Mac that joins the iPhone's folder brings saves it keeps elsewhere already, not a game played on. The switches waited 30 to 45 seconds, the time the library takes to be read again from a NAS, before they moved.
